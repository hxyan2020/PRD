Problem

A market-making desk quotes two-sided markets and accumulates inventory. Risk must keep delta within limits, skew quotes to mean-revert inventory, and separate genuine spread capture from directional risk.

Approach

  1. Simulate two-sided quoting against a flow model, accumulating net inventory per token.
  2. Track real-time delta and inventory-limit utilisation with soft/hard limits.
  3. Implement quote-skewing that widens/tightens sides to pull inventory toward neutral.
  4. Hedge residual delta via perpetuals and compute hedging cost and basis risk.
  5. Attribute P&L across spread capture, inventory mark-to-market and hedging, and add a fat-tailed inventory VaR.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Inventory/delta monitor with quote-skew logic
  • P&L attribution (spread vs. inventory vs. hedge)
  • Inventory VaR and limit-breach alerts

Show in interview

  • Net delta and inventory-limit utilisation
  • P&L attribution: spread vs. inventory vs. hedge
  • Inventory VaR (fat-tailed)
  • Average inventory distance from neutral

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The market-making desk continuously accumulates directional inventory across dozens of spot and perpetual pairs while quoting two-sided markets 24/7. Without a real-time view of net inventory, its dollar VaR, and how quote skew is compensating for risk, the desk can drift into concentrated, correlated exposure that a single 15-minute volatility spike can turn into an outsized loss before any human intervenes.

Objective

Deliver a real-time inventory risk engine that computes per-asset and portfolio net inventory, EWMA realised-volatility-based intraday VaR, and a recommended quote skew that mechanically leans quotes to flatten inventory as it approaches soft and hard limits, all refreshed sub-second and enforced 24/7.

Users

Market makers / desk traders managing live quote parametersDesk head / head of trading setting risk appetite and limitsMiddle-office market risk analysts monitoring intraday VaRQuant developers calibrating the skew and volatility models

Data inputs

  • Real-time fills and open orders from all trading venues and the internal OMS
  • Level-1/Level-2 market data (mid, bid/ask, depth) per instrument
  • Reference data: contract multipliers, tick size, funding schedule, asset correlation groups
  • EWMA realised volatility inputs (1-minute log returns) per asset
  • Risk configuration: soft/hard/kill inventory limits, VaR appetite, confidence level, skew coefficients
  • Funding rates and basis for perpetual-vs-spot delta netting

Functional requirements

  • Aggregate fills from all venues and internal OMS into a real-time net inventory position per asset, netting spot, perpetual, and hedges into a single delta-equivalent notional.
  • Compute intraday VaR per asset and at portfolio level using an EWMA (lambda ~0.94) volatility estimate on 1-minute log returns, applying a correlation matrix refreshed at least hourly, at 99% confidence over a 1-hour horizon.
  • Derive a recommended quote skew (bid/ask offset in bps) as a monotonic function of inventory utilisation so quotes lean to attract flow that flattens the book.
  • Enforce three-tier inventory limits (soft warn, hard, kill) per asset and at portfolio VaR level, auto-widening spreads at hard and pulling one side of the quote at kill.
  • Provide a real-time dashboard showing net inventory, utilisation %, realised vol, VaR contribution, and current applied skew per asset with drill-down.
  • Emit limit-breach and stale-market-data alerts to desk and middle office with full context and acknowledgement workflow.
  • Log every skew change, limit breach, and auto-hedge trigger to an immutable audit trail for post-trade and control review.

Non-functional

  • End-to-end latency from fill to updated inventory/VaR/skew under 250ms at p99 to remain relevant in fast markets.
  • Continuous 24/7 availability with graceful degradation to a conservative (max-skew, widened) safe mode on market-data loss.
  • Deterministic, reproducible VaR: identical inputs must produce identical outputs for audit and back-testing.
  • Horizontal scalability to 200+ instruments without breaching the latency budget.

Success metrics

  • Intraday VaR limit breaches reduced by >60% quarter-on-quarter without material P&L drag.
  • Median inventory holding time reduced by 30% via skew-driven mean reversion of the book.
  • Zero undetected limit breaches persisting beyond one refresh cycle (measured against reconciliation).
  • Skew engine attribution: >70% of inventory flattening explained by skew-induced flow rather than manual hedges.
  • VaR back-test exception rate within the 99% confidence band (Kupiec test pass).
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Operate the live inventory/VaR loop so every fill updates delta, skew, and hedges within appetite on a continuous 24/7 quoting desk.

Frequency

Continuous, event-driven on every fill and mark-price tick; session handovers only for health checks, not for risk decisions.

1

On each venue/OMS fill, update net inventory and delta-equivalent exposure (spot + perp netted).

Owner: Inventory risk engine

Control: Fill-to-inventory latency SLO monitored; stale positions block quoting until reconciled.

2

Refresh EWMA vol and intraday VaR; recompute recommended quote skew from inventory utilisation.

Owner: Inventory risk engine

Control: Identical inputs must produce identical VaR/skew; safe mode on stale market data.

3

Push skew to the quoting engine; auto-widen or pull a quote side when hard/kill limits fire.

Owner: Quoting engine / on-shift MM

Control: Hard/kill actions are system-enforced; overrides require desk-head dual auth.

4

If inventory cannot mean-revert via skew alone, trigger algorithmic or manual hedge to flatten.

Owner: On-shift market maker

Control: Hedge tickets linked to the breach event ID; audit trail immutable.

5

Acknowledge soft-warn and hard/kill alerts with rationale; notify middle office on kill or portfolio VaR breach.

Owner: On-shift MM / market risk analyst

Control: Soft-warn rationale within 5 minutes; kill events open a mandatory post-event ticket.

6

At each global session handover, verify engine health and spot-check inventory vs venue/OMS.

Owner: On-shift market maker

Control: Reconciliation break >0.5% notional blocks the handover until cleared.

Escalation

Soft-warn stays on-desk with logged rationale. Hard breach notifies the desk head in real time. Kill, portfolio VaR breach, or safe-mode persistence escalates to head of trading and head of market risk; if inventory cannot flatten within 30 minutes, engage the CRO and halt quoting.
