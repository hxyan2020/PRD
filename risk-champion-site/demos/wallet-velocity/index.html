Problem

A sell-side venue must detect suspicious deposit/withdrawal patterns — rapid pass-through, mixer interaction, sanctioned-address exposure — to meet AML/Travel Rule obligations and protect the venue.

Approach

  1. Ingest deposit/withdrawal flows and enrich with on-chain address labels and risk scores.
  2. Compute wallet-velocity metrics (turnover speed, pass-through ratio, fan-in/fan-out).
  3. Screen against sanctioned addresses, mixers and high-risk clusters.
  4. Score accounts and generate prioritised AML alerts with case context.
  5. Feed confirmed cases into suspicious-activity reporting and account-restriction workflows.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Wallet-velocity and pass-through monitor
  • Sanctions/mixer screening with risk scores
  • Prioritised AML alert queue and case files

Show in interview

  • Wallet-velocity / pass-through ratio distribution
  • Sanctions / mixer exposure alerts
  • Account risk-score ranking
  • Alert-to-case conversion and false-positive rate

Stack: Python · On-chain APIs / SQL · Screening data · Dashboard

Sample talk track

One account showed classic layering — funds deposited and withdrawn within minutes to fresh addresses with high fan-out and a hop from a mixer. My velocity and pass-through metrics scored it red, the screening confirmed mixer exposure, and it went straight to a suspicious-activity case with an automatic withdrawal hold, which is exactly the Travel-Rule-aligned control MAS expects.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Customer withdrawals settle on-chain 24/7 and can be irreversible within minutes, yet the exchange must keep only a small fraction of assets in hot wallets to limit theft exposure. Without automated hot-wallet caps and withdrawal-velocity controls, a compromised account, key, or API can drain the hot wallet before operations reacts, and legitimate demand spikes can exhaust the hot balance and force customer-facing delays.

Objective

Build a wallet risk controller that enforces per-asset hot-wallet balance caps, monitors rolling 24-hour withdrawal velocity per user and exchange-wide, routes withdrawals through auto-approval or manual/multi-sig review based on risk scoring, and schedules cold-wallet sweeps and top-ups to keep hot balances inside target bands.

Users

Treasury / wallet operations team managing hot and cold balancesFinancial crime / compliance analysts reviewing flagged withdrawalsSecurity engineers owning multi-sig and key custody controlsRisk managers setting velocity thresholds and hot-wallet caps

Data inputs

  • Withdrawal request stream (user, asset, amount, destination address, memo/tag)
  • Real-time hot and cold wallet balances per asset and per chain
  • KYT / blockchain-analytics risk scores for destination addresses
  • User KYC tier, account age, historical withdrawal pattern, and device/session risk signals
  • Travel Rule counterparty VASP data for transfers above threshold
  • Configuration: hot-wallet caps, velocity thresholds, approval limits, sweep target bands

Functional requirements

  • Enforce configurable per-asset hot-wallet caps as a percentage of total custody, blocking or queuing withdrawals that would breach the cap.
  • Compute rolling 24-hour withdrawal velocity per user, per asset, and exchange-wide, comparing against dynamic thresholds and historical baselines.
  • Score each withdrawal (amount, destination reputation via KYT, address age, account history, Travel Rule status) and route to auto-approve, manual review, or hold.
  • Require multi-sig / dual approval for any single withdrawal above a threshold or for cumulative velocity breaches.
  • Trigger cold-to-hot top-ups and hot-to-cold sweeps automatically to keep hot balances within target bands, with all cold movements requiring multi-sig.
  • Integrate KYT screening and Travel Rule data collection for cross-VASP transfers above the applicable threshold.
  • Provide an operations console showing hot/cold balances, pending withdrawals, velocity heatmaps, and a full approval/audit log.

Non-functional

  • Withdrawal risk decision returned within 2 seconds for auto-approve path to preserve customer experience.
  • Fail-closed design: any control-system outage or ambiguous state defaults to manual review, never auto-release.
  • Tamper-evident, append-only audit log of every approval, threshold change, and cold-wallet movement.
  • 24/7 availability with on-call coverage aligned to global withdrawal volume patterns.

Success metrics

  • Hot-wallet balance held within target band >95% of the time, minimising both theft exposure and withdrawal-liquidity incidents.
  • Median auto-approved withdrawal decision latency under 2 seconds.
  • Zero unauthorised withdrawals released via the auto-approval path (measured by post-incident review).
  • False-positive manual-review rate below 5% of total withdrawals to protect customer experience.
  • 100% of above-threshold cross-VASP transfers with complete Travel Rule data before release.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Keep hot/cold balances inside policy bands and gate every withdrawal through velocity and anomaly controls before irreversible on-chain release.

Frequency

Continuous automated enforcement on every withdrawal and balance tick; treasury band reviews at each shift handover.

1

Read live hot and cold balances and remaining hot-wallet headroom per asset/chain.

Owner: Wallet risk controller

Control: Fail-closed if balance feeds are stale or ambiguous—queue, never auto-release.

2

On withdrawal request, score KYT, Travel Rule completeness, account risk, and rolling 24h velocity (user + exchange-wide).

Owner: Wallet risk controller

Control: Any KYT hit, missing Travel Rule data, or velocity breach routes to manual/multi-sig.

3

Auto-approve low-risk, within-cap withdrawals; freeze or hold anomalous / high-velocity flows.

Owner: Wallet risk controller

Control: Auto-path limited to pre-set amount and risk bands; every release logged.

4

Work the review queue: verify destination and account behaviour; approve under multi-sig, reject, or freeze the account.

Owner: Financial crime analyst / wallet ops

Control: Four-eyes on manual decisions; M-of-N multi-sig for large or velocity-flagged releases.

5

When hot balance exits the target band, execute multi-sig cold-to-hot top-up or hot-to-cold sweep.

Owner: Treasury / wallet operations

Control: Cold movements require multi-sig and dual sign-off; amounts reconciled to bands.

6

At shift handover, reconcile on-chain balances to the ledger and clear outstanding velocity alerts.

Owner: Treasury shift lead

Control: Balance reconciliation break blocks handover sign-off.

Escalation

High-risk KYT hits or suspected account compromise escalate to the financial-crime lead and may freeze withdrawals on the account. Exchange-wide velocity spikes or anomalous hot-wallet drawdowns trigger a global withdrawal pause and notify head of security, head of compliance, and CRO. Suspected key/multi-sig compromise invokes the security incident-response runbook.
