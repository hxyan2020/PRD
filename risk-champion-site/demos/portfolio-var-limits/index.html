Problem

A multi-asset fund needs a defensible daily view of market risk with limits by desk and factor, and clear escalation when utilisation approaches thresholds.

Approach

  1. Assemble a synthetic multi-asset portfolio (equities, rates, FX, credit) with position-level risk-factor mapping.
  2. Implement historical-simulation and Monte Carlo VaR plus expected shortfall, with configurable confidence and horizon.
  3. Compute component/marginal VaR to attribute risk to positions and factors.
  4. Layer a limit framework with utilisation, breach flags and early-warning triggers, and backtest VaR with a traffic-light test.
  5. Visualise everything in an interactive dashboard with drill-down by desk and factor.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Interactive risk dashboard with VaR/ES and limit utilisation
  • Backtesting report with exception analysis
  • Documented methodology note comparing HS vs. Monte Carlo

Show in interview

  • 1-day 99% VaR and ES by desk
  • Component VaR concentration (top-N contributors)
  • Backtesting exceptions vs. expected
  • Limit utilisation % and breach count

Stack: Python · pandas / numpy · Plotly / Streamlit or Next.js · SQL

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Portfolio managers run multi-asset books whose aggregate market risk is opaque intraday, and the risk desk lacks a single, model-transparent view of Value-at-Risk, Expected Shortfall and limit utilisation. Ad-hoc spreadsheets produce inconsistent confidence levels, holding periods and lookback windows, so breaches are detected late and cannot be traced to the positions or risk factors driving them.

Objective

Deliver a daily VaR and limit-monitoring engine that computes 1-day and 10-day 99% historical-simulation and Monte Carlo VaR/ES, decomposes risk via component/marginal VaR, tracks utilisation against a governed limit hierarchy, and evidences model validity through Kupiec and Christoffersen backtests.

Users

Market risk analysts running the T+1 risk packPortfolio managers checking pre-trade limit headroomChief Risk Officer and the Risk CommitteeIndependent model validation / model risk teamFund oversight and compliance monitoring

Data inputs

  • End-of-day positions and lots by instrument, book and legal entity from the OMS/IBOR
  • Cleansed market data: price/yield/vol histories, curves, surfaces and FX rates from the golden source
  • Instrument static and terms (identifiers, multipliers, sensitivities, cashflow schedules)
  • Factor covariance / correlation matrices and EWMA decay parameters
  • Governed limit hierarchy and threshold configuration
  • Independently sourced daily clean and dirty P&L for backtesting

Functional requirements

  • Compute 1-day and 10-day VaR and Expected Shortfall at 95% and 99% using full-revaluation historical simulation over a 2-year (≈520 observation) rolling window with age/volatility weighting configurable per book.
  • Compute Monte Carlo VaR/ES from a factor covariance matrix (EWMA λ=0.94 and equal-weight variants) with correlated Gaussian and Student-t innovations, minimum 25,000 antithetic paths.
  • Produce component VaR, marginal VaR and incremental VaR so total risk sums exactly to the reported portfolio VaR, sliceable by asset class, desk, strategy, issuer and risk factor.
  • Enforce a hierarchical limit framework (firm > fund > strategy > desk) with hard limits, soft/warning thresholds and utilisation percentages, flagging amber at 80% and red on breach.
  • Run automated VaR backtesting: clean and dirty P&L versus VaR, exception counting, Kupiec POF and Christoffersen independence tests, and a rolling traffic-light (Basel) zone status.
  • Support pre-trade what-if incremental VaR so a PM can test a hypothetical trade against remaining limit headroom before execution.
  • Generate an auditable daily risk pack (PDF/dashboard) with sign-off workflow, versioned inputs and full parameter provenance.

Non-functional

  • Complete the full-book overnight VaR batch within the T+1 risk window (target < 45 minutes) and return pre-trade what-if VaR in under 5 seconds.
  • Full reproducibility: every number reconstructable from immutable snapshots of positions, market data, seeds and model parameters.
  • Segregation of duties and role-based access so limit parameters can be viewed by PMs but only changed via governed four-eyes workflow.
  • Model transparency: all methodology, assumptions and approximations documented to satisfy independent model validation and audit.

Success metrics

  • VaR batch completes before the 07:30 risk-pack deadline on ≥ 99% of business days
  • Backtest exceptions remain within the 99% green zone (≤ 4 exceptions per 250 days) and pass Kupiec/Christoffersen at 5%
  • 100% of limit breaches detected, escalated and evidenced same-day with attribution to driving positions
  • Component VaR reconciles to total portfolio VaR within a 0.5% tolerance
  • Pre-trade what-if VaR adopted for ≥ 90% of limit-sensitive trades
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Run the overnight T+1 VaR/ES batch, backtest against independent P&L, and remediate any limit or model exceptions before the morning risk pack is signed.

Frequency

Overnight T+1 batch each business day (target pack by 07:30); weekly Kupiec/Christoffersen deep-dive; monthly parameter recalibration with model validation.

1

After EOD close, lock IBOR positions and golden-source market data into an immutable snapshot; abort if feed completeness or staleness checks fail.

Owner: Market risk ops / overnight batch owner

Control: Hard-stop on incomplete feeds; snapshot checksum and parameter version logged before engines start.

2

Execute historical-simulation and Monte Carlo VaR/ES at governed confidence levels and holding periods; produce component and marginal VaR.

Owner: Market risk analyst

Control: Run ID ties to snapshot hash; engine failure pages the on-call analyst before the risk-pack deadline.

3

Map utilisation against the firm > fund > strategy > desk limit tree and open amber (≥80%) / red (breach) items with book- and factor-level attribution.

Owner: Market risk analyst

Control: System-enforced thresholds; every red item lands in the breach register with same-day owner.

4

Backtest prior-day VaR versus independently sourced clean and dirty P&L; count exceptions and refresh Kupiec POF, Christoffersen independence and Basel traffic-light zone.

Owner: Model validation liaison

Control: Independent P&L source; exception root-cause coded (data, model, genuine market move).

5

If a hard limit is breached, notify the PM, agree de-risking or temporary limit relief, and record target close date in the breach register.

Owner: Risk manager with portfolio manager

Control: Four-eyes on remediation plan; CRO visibility on any item ageing past the policy window.

6

Sign and distribute the daily risk pack; keep pre-trade what-if VaR available for PMs during the trading day.

Owner: Risk manager

Control: Electronic sign-off with timestamp; intraday what-if uses the same locked overnight parameter set.

Escalation

Amber utilisation is notified to the PM and desk head for monitoring. Hard breaches escalate immediately to the CRO; unresolved same-day items go to the Risk Committee with a possible de-risking mandate. Amber/red backtest zones escalate to model validation and may force a VaR multiplier or formal model review.
