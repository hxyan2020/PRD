Problem

Crypto funds run cash-and-carry and funding-capture trades that look riskless but carry funding-flip, basis-blowout and venue risk. Risk must monitor the true risk-adjusted carry.

Approach

  1. Collect perpetual funding rates, futures basis and spot across venues in real time.
  2. Compute annualised carry for cash-and-carry and funding-capture strategies net of fees.
  3. Model funding-rate regime shifts and basis convergence/divergence risk.
  4. Overlay margin and distance-to-liquidation on the short-perp leg under a price spike.
  5. Produce a risk-adjusted carry metric and venue concentration view.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Funding & basis monitor across venues
  • Risk-adjusted carry calculator
  • Liquidation-overlay on the hedge leg

Show in interview

  • Annualised carry (gross vs net vs risk-adjusted)
  • Funding-rate regime & flip risk
  • Distance-to-liquidation on hedge leg
  • Venue concentration of the trade

Sample talk track

The headline carry was ~12% annualised, but once I netted fees and stressed a funding flip plus a 20% spot spike squeezing the short-perp margin, the risk-adjusted carry halved and one venue held 60% of the exposure. That reframes 'free carry' as a funding-and-liquidation risk position that needs sizing and venue diversification.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The fund runs cash-and-carry and funding-capture strategies — long spot / short perp, or basis trades against dated futures — to harvest funding and basis. Returns look attractive on paper but the desk lacks a consolidated, risk-adjusted view: funding can flip negative, basis can compress or invert, and a spot squeeze can force perp-leg liquidation, turning a 'market-neutral' carry into a directional loss. There is no single monitor tying funding, basis, carry and the liquidation overlay together.

Objective

Build a perp funding and cash-and-carry basis risk monitor that tracks funding rates and futures basis across venues, computes risk-adjusted carry net of costs, and overlays liquidation risk on the short-perp leg so the desk can size, roll and unwind carry trades safely 24/7.

Users

Basis/carry strategy portfolio managerRisk managerTrading and execution deskCIO / investment committee for strategy limits

Data inputs

  • Real-time and predicted perp funding rates and historical funding series per venue
  • Dated futures prices and expiries for basis and term-structure computation
  • Spot prices and the fund's paired spot inventory / borrow costs
  • Per-venue margin balances, maintenance margin tiers and mark prices for the short-perp leg
  • Trading fee schedules, borrow rates and order-book depth for slippage estimates
  • Historical funding/basis data for volatility estimation and backtesting

Functional requirements

  • Track real-time perp funding rates (current and predicted next), historical funding, and annualized funding across venues per asset.
  • Compute futures basis for dated contracts (annualized), spot-perp basis, and term structure across expiries and venues.
  • Compute risk-adjusted carry: gross funding/basis carry net of trading fees, borrow, slippage and expected funding volatility, expressed as an annualized, risk-adjusted return.
  • Overlay liquidation risk on the short-perp leg: margin usage, distance-to-liquidation and required buffer given the paired spot leg, including a spot-squeeze scenario.
  • Support position construction and sizing that respects per-venue margin, funding-volatility budget and the liquidation overlay, with roll scheduling around expiries and funding boundaries.
  • Alert on funding sign flips, basis inversion/compression beyond thresholds, negative risk-adjusted carry, and liquidation-buffer breaches, 24/7.
  • Backtest carry P&L attribution (funding vs basis vs execution vs liquidation events) and report realized vs expected carry.

Non-functional

  • Refresh funding/basis and margin data within seconds around funding settlement and expiry windows.
  • Cost and slippage assumptions versioned and conservative so risk-adjusted carry is not overstated.
  • Reliable multi-venue connectivity with reconciliation of both legs; flag any leg desync.
  • Auditable P&L attribution and assumption history for committee review.

Success metrics

  • Realized carry within an acceptable band of expected risk-adjusted carry in backtests
  • Zero carry positions carried into negative risk-adjusted territory without documented approval
  • Liquidation-buffer breaches on the short leg detected and remediated within target SLA, 24/7
  • Funding sign-flip and basis-inversion alerts delivered before the next funding settlement
  • Improved Sharpe / reduced drawdown of the carry book vs pre-tool baseline
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Jointly monitor funding rates and futures basis against short-leg liquidation risk so carry trades are sized only when risk-adjusted carry is positive and buffers are adequate — and halted or unwound when carry flips or liquidation risk spikes.

Frequency

Continuous 24/7 monitoring with intensified refresh around each 8-hour funding settlement and ahead of every dated-contract expiry/roll.

1

Refresh funding rates (current and predicted), dated basis, spot inventory, short-leg margin and cost inputs across venues.

Owner: Carry monitor / venue ops

Control: Both legs reconciled; stale funding feed or leg desync halts new sizing.

2

Compute annualized funding, basis term structure and risk-adjusted carry net of fees, borrow and slippage.

Owner: Carry monitor

Control: Cost/vol assumptions from the approved versioned set only.

3

Overlay liquidation risk on the short-perp leg, including a spot-squeeze scenario against the paired spot.

Owner: Risk analyst

Control: Distance-to-liquidation must clear the required buffer for the paired notional.

4

Decide size, hold, roll or halt: positive carry with adequate buffer may size/add; negative carry or thin buffer blocks adds.

Owner: Portfolio manager / risk manager

Control: Limit breach blocks adds and flags the book for roll or unwind.

5

At funding settlement or pre-expiry, instruct the desk to size, roll or halt/unwind both legs.

Owner: Portfolio manager → trading desk

Control: Four-eyes execution; both legs adjusted atomically where the venue allows.

6

Confirm neutrality, buffers and attribution of realized carry (funding vs basis vs execution).

Owner: Trading desk / risk analyst

Control: Residual directional risk or unexplained attribution variance escalated.

Escalation

Amber (risk-adjusted carry approaching zero, basis compressing, or buffer within 1.5× of trigger) notifies risk manager and PM to plan a roll or trim. Red (funding sign flip with negative carry, basis inversion, or short-leg liquidation imminent from a spot squeeze) pages the head of risk and CIO and authorizes immediate halt/unwind under the pre-approved playbook.
