Problem

Before allocating to a DeFi lending or yield protocol, risk must assess whether the advertised yield is sustainable and whether the protocol carries hidden smart-contract, oracle or governance risk.

Approach

  1. Define scoring dimensions: audit maturity, oracle design, TVL depth/concentration, admin-key/timelock governance, and yield sustainability.
  2. Pull on-chain TVL, oracle and governance parameters and combine with audit/bug-bounty history.
  3. Separate real yield (fees) from token-emission-subsidised yield and project decay.
  4. Apply red-flag gates (unaudited, single-key admin, manipulable oracle) that veto allocation regardless of score.
  5. Output a weighted score, position-size recommendation and monitoring triggers.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Reusable DeFi protocol risk scorecard
  • Yield-sustainability decomposition (real vs. emissions)
  • Red-flag gate checklist and monitoring triggers

Show in interview

  • Composite protocol risk score by dimension
  • Real yield vs. emissions-subsidised yield
  • Admin-key / governance risk flags
  • Recommended position size and monitoring triggers

Sample talk track

One protocol showed a headline 24% APY, but my decomposition revealed 20 points came from token emissions decaying within months, and its oracle was a single spot-price feed vulnerable to flash-loan manipulation. That combination hit two red-flag gates, so it was a no regardless of the attractive score — exactly the kind of trap the scorecard exists to catch.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The fund deploys capital into DeFi protocols (lending, DEXs, LSTs, perps, vaults) chasing yield, but protocol risk is assessed ad-hoc. Smart-contract exploits, oracle manipulation, upgradeable admin keys and governance attacks have each caused total loss of principal. There is no standardized, comparable risk rating to decide whether a protocol is investable, at what size, and what conditions would force an exit.

Objective

Build a DeFi protocol risk scorecard that scores each protocol across smart-contract, oracle, governance/admin-key, economic and liquidity dimensions, rolls them into a composite rating tied to position-size limits, and continuously monitors on-chain signals for deterioration.

Users

DeFi strategy portfolio managerRisk manager / protocol due-diligence leadInvestment committee approving new protocol allowlistsOperations team managing on-chain positions and keys

Data inputs

  • Audit reports, bug-bounty terms and known exploit databases
  • On-chain contract metadata: proxy/upgradeability, admin/multisig configuration, timelock parameters
  • Oracle configuration and live oracle feeds for deviation monitoring
  • TVL, utilization, collateral composition and liquidity depth (protocol + DEX analytics)
  • Governance token distribution, voting power concentration and active proposals
  • Historical incident and rug-pull datasets for calibration

Functional requirements

  • Score smart-contract risk from audit count/quality, time-in-production, bug-bounty size, code immutability, and exploit history, with evidence links.
  • Assess oracle risk: oracle type (Chainlink, TWAP, custom), number of sources, update frequency, manipulation resistance and single-point-of-failure exposure.
  • Assess governance and admin-key risk: multisig threshold and signer set, timelock presence/length, upgradeability, and concentration of governance token voting power.
  • Assess economic and liquidity risk: TVL and TVL trend, utilization, collateral quality, liquidity depth for exit, and dependency on incentive emissions.
  • Combine dimension scores into a weighted composite rating (e.g. A-E) that maps to a maximum position size and required collateralization/monitoring intensity.
  • Continuously monitor on-chain signals (TVL drawdown, large admin transactions, timelock queued changes, oracle deviation, governance proposals) and re-rate on material change.
  • Maintain an auditable allowlist with per-protocol limits, approval dates, reviewers and exit conditions.

Non-functional

  • Scorecard methodology is versioned and transparent so ratings are reproducible and defensible to the committee.
  • On-chain monitoring runs 24/7 with alerting on admin-key and timelock events within minutes of block confirmation.
  • Chain-agnostic architecture supporting the major EVM chains and key non-EVM chains in scope.
  • Segregation of duties between rating (risk) and deployment (PM/ops); read-only analytics separate from signing keys.

Success metrics

  • 100% of allowlisted protocols carry a current composite rating with linked evidence
  • Zero capital deployed to protocols below the committee-approved minimum rating
  • Material adverse on-chain events (admin change, oracle deviation, TVL flush) detected and alerted within target SLA
  • Position sizes remain within the size band mandated by each protocol's rating in >99% of monitored time
  • Loss-avoidance track record: exits executed ahead of at least the majority of monitored deterioration events
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Score DeFi protocols on audit, oracle, TVL and governance dimensions, gate capital behind a red-flag check, and size or block deployment so only adequately rated protocols receive risk-budgeted capital.

Frequency

Full scorecard before onboarding and at least quarterly thereafter; continuous 24/7 on-chain monitoring of admin keys, timelocks, oracles and TVL with immediate re-rating on material change.

1

Collect audit evidence, oracle configuration, TVL/liquidity metrics and governance/admin-key setup for the protocol.

Owner: Protocol due-diligence / on-chain analyst

Control: Every input linked to a source; missing evidence caps the achievable dimension score.

2

Score audit, oracle, TVL/economic and governance dimensions; compute the weighted composite rating.

Owner: DeFi risk scorecard engine

Control: Scoring uses the current versioned methodology; overrides require written justification.

3

Run the red-flag gate (unaudited critical code, single-key admin, manipulable oracle, or TVL flush history).

Owner: Risk manager

Control: Any hard red flag blocks onboarding regardless of composite score.

4

Map the passing rating to a maximum position size and monitoring intensity; seek committee allowlist approval.

Owner: Risk manager → investment committee

Control: Approval recorded with reviewers, date, size band and exit conditions.

5

Deploy via multisig within the approved size band, or block/reject if below the investable threshold.

Owner: Portfolio manager / on-chain ops

Control: Signing segregated from rating; deployments above band are system-blocked.

6

Monitor on-chain admin, timelock, oracle and TVL signals 24/7; re-rate on material change.

Owner: On-chain monitoring engine

Control: Triggered exit condition forces immediate review and staged unwind.

7

If rating falls below threshold or a red flag fires, execute the staged exit playbook to zero residual.

Owner: Risk manager → on-chain ops

Control: Unwind follows the pre-approved playbook; residual exposure tracked until cleared.

Escalation

A one-notch downgrade or amber on-chain signal notifies the risk manager and PM. A red-flag gate failure, downgrade below the minimum investable rating, queued malicious admin/timelock change, active oracle deviation or sharp TVL flush pages the head of risk and CIO and triggers the immediate exit playbook, prioritizing withdrawal of principal even at a cost.
