Problem

An exchange must prove it holds client assets 1:1 and keeps them segregated from house funds. Risk/finance must reconcile on-chain reserves to internal liabilities and produce an attestation.

Approach

  1. Enumerate the exchange's hot and cold wallets and pull live on-chain balances per asset.
  2. Aggregate client liabilities from the internal ledger and build a Merkle tree of user balances.
  3. Reconcile total reserves vs total liabilities per asset and compute the coverage ratio.
  4. Verify house-vs-client segregation and flag any commingling or shortfall.
  5. Generate a proof-of-reserves report with Merkle root for user-verifiable inclusion.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Reserves-vs-liabilities reconciliation per asset
  • Merkle-tree proof-of-reserves with user inclusion proofs
  • Segregation & coverage-ratio attestation report

Show in interview

  • Reserve-vs-liability coverage ratio per asset
  • Segregation breaches / commingling flags
  • Merkle root & inclusion-proof coverage
  • Cold-vs-hot custody split

Sample talk track

I reconciled on-chain reserves against ledger liabilities per asset and wrapped user balances in a Merkle tree so any customer can verify inclusion without exposing the whole book. The coverage ratio has to sit at or above 100% per asset, and the segregation check flags any client funds sitting in house wallets — the exact failure mode that sank FTX.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Customers and regulators require assurance that the exchange holds sufficient reserves to cover all customer liabilities, that customer assets are segregated from corporate funds, and that operational keys are protected via hot/cold segregation and multi-sig. Without a rigorous, repeatable proof-of-reserves process, the exchange cannot credibly demonstrate solvency or detect a reserves shortfall before it becomes a crisis.

Objective

Implement a proof-of-reserves and wallet-segregation attestation system that snapshots on-chain reserves, builds a Merkle tree of customer liabilities enabling individual inclusion proofs, computes the reserve coverage ratio per asset, verifies hot/cold and customer/corporate segregation, and produces attestations for auditors and the public.

Users

Finance / treasury team owning reserves and the balance sheetInternal audit and external attestation auditorsCompliance and regulatory-reporting officersCustomers verifying their own inclusion in the liability tree

Data inputs

  • On-chain reserve address list per asset with ownership-proof signatures
  • Real-time and snapshot on-chain balances per address and chain
  • Internal ledger of customer liabilities (balances owed) per asset at cut-off
  • Hot/cold segregation policy and current wallet classification
  • Multi-sig configuration and signer set for cold custody
  • Reference prices for aggregating multi-asset coverage into a common unit

Functional requirements

  • Snapshot all on-chain reserve addresses (hot, warm, cold) per asset at a point in time with cryptographic proof of address control.
  • Construct a Merkle tree of customer liabilities so each customer can verify inclusion of their balance without exposing other customers' data.
  • Compute per-asset and aggregate reserve coverage ratio (reserves / liabilities) and flag any asset below 100%.
  • Verify segregation: customer assets held separately from corporate/operational funds, and hot-wallet balances within policy caps versus cold storage.
  • Validate custody controls: multi-sig configuration on cold wallets and address-ownership signatures for each reserve address.
  • Publish an attestation report and a customer-facing tool for inclusion-proof verification.
  • Retain immutable historical snapshots to evidence coverage over time and support auditor review.

Non-functional

  • Point-in-time consistency: reserves snapshot and liabilities snapshot must be aligned to the same block/height and ledger cut-off.
  • Privacy-preserving liability proofs that do not leak individual balances or customer counts beyond intended disclosure.
  • Tamper-evident, reproducible attestation artefacts verifiable by an independent third party.
  • Ability to complete a full attestation run within the agreed reporting window (e.g. monthly).

Success metrics

  • Reserve coverage ratio >=100% per material asset at every attestation, with any shortfall detected before publication.
  • 100% of reserve addresses evidenced by valid ownership signatures at snapshot.
  • Customer inclusion-proof verification success rate of 100% for sampled customers.
  • Attestation run completed within the reporting window with zero reconciliation breaks between ledger and on-chain snapshot.
  • Successful independent third-party reproduction of published attestation results.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Run a point-in-time proof-of-reserves attestation: wallet snapshot, liabilities reconciliation, coverage check, and public publish.

Frequency

Daily attestation run (aligned block height + ledger cut-off), with continuous coverage monitoring between publishes.

1

Freeze the ledger cut-off and snapshot all reserve wallet balances at the aligned block height.

Owner: Treasury + blockchain engineer

Control: Ledger and on-chain cut-offs must match; block heights and timestamps recorded.

2

Prove control of each reserve address and confirm cold-wallet multi-sig configuration.

Owner: Security engineer

Control: Unsigned addresses excluded and flagged; multi-sig config verified against policy.

3

Build the Merkle tree of customer liabilities and reconcile leaf sum to the ledger liability total.

Owner: Attestation engineer

Control: Merkle root recomputed independently; leaf-sum break blocks publish.

4

Compute per-asset coverage (reserves / liabilities) and verify hot/cold and customer/corporate segregation.

Owner: Finance analyst / internal audit

Control: Sub-100% coverage or commingling blocks publication pending remediation.

5

Publish the attestation report and customer inclusion-proof tool; archive immutable artefacts.

Owner: Compliance officer

Control: Finance, audit, and compliance sign-off required before release.

Escalation

Coverage below 100%, unsigned reserve addresses, ledger-to-chain breaks, or customer/corporate commingling escalate immediately to CFO, head of internal audit, and CRO; publication halts until remediated. Suspected reserve shortfall invokes the solvency-incident runbook with board and, where required, regulator notification.
