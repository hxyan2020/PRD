Problem

Crypto OTC settles bilaterally without a CCP, so a counterparty can fail after you've paid. Risk must set credit lines, require pre-funding where needed, and control settlement (DvP) risk.

Approach

  1. Score OTC counterparties and set credit lines by tier, tenor and product.
  2. Monitor real-time line utilisation across open trades and pre-settlement exposure.
  3. Model settlement risk for bilateral trades and enforce DvP / atomic-settlement or pre-funding rules.
  4. Stress a counterparty default at settlement and quantify replacement-cost loss.
  5. Trigger escalations (reduce line, require collateral/pre-fund) as utilisation or risk rises.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Counterparty credit-line framework & utilisation monitor
  • DvP / pre-funding settlement-risk controls
  • Default-at-settlement stress report

Show in interview

  • Credit-line utilisation by counterparty
  • Pre-settlement vs settlement exposure
  • Settlement-default replacement-cost loss
  • % of flow settled DvP / pre-funded

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The OTC desk trades large bilateral blocks with institutional counterparties, extending settlement credit or relying on pre-funding. In a market that settles on-chain 24/7 with irreversible transfers, unmanaged counterparty credit lines and settlement timing gaps expose the desk to the risk that a counterparty defaults after the desk has delivered but before it has received value.

Objective

Build an OTC counterparty credit and settlement risk system that assigns credit tiers and limits, tracks real-time line utilisation and current exposure (including mark-to-market on open trades), enforces DvP or pre-funding policies by counterparty tier, and stress-tests settlement default scenarios to size potential loss.

Users

OTC desk traders and salesCredit risk officers setting and monitoring counterparty linesSettlement operations processing DvP and pre-fundingRisk managers running settlement default stress tests

Data inputs

  • Counterparty static data: KYC/KYB, credit rating, tier, approved limits
  • Open and settled OTC trade blotter with notional, direction, and price
  • Real-time mark-to-market prices for open trade exposure
  • On-chain and fiat settlement status and confirmation/finality data
  • Collateral / pre-funding balances held per counterparty
  • Stress-scenario parameters: price shocks, default assumptions, recovery rates

Functional requirements

  • Assign each counterparty a credit tier and limit based on KYC/KYB, financials, and internal rating, with periodic review.
  • Track real-time credit-line utilisation combining unsettled trade notional and current mark-to-market exposure.
  • Enforce settlement policy by tier: DvP/atomic settlement or full pre-funding for lower tiers, net settlement windows for higher tiers.
  • Block or require approval for trades that would breach a counterparty's credit limit or concentration limit.
  • Monitor settlement status in real time and flag late or partial settlements against expected value receipt.
  • Run settlement-default stress tests estimating loss given a counterparty failing to deliver under stressed prices.
  • Provide a credit dashboard showing per-counterparty exposure, utilisation, settlement pipeline, and stress results.

Non-functional

  • Real-time exposure updates as trades are struck and marked, available before the next trade is confirmed.
  • Fail-safe settlement: ambiguous settlement states default to holding delivery rather than releasing value.
  • Auditable credit decisions and limit changes with dual authorisation.
  • 24/7 settlement monitoring aligned to on-chain finality times per asset.

Success metrics

  • Zero settlement losses from counterparty default on DvP/pre-funded flows.
  • 100% of trades checked against credit and concentration limits before execution.
  • Credit-line utilisation kept within approved limits >99% of the time, with breaches approved and time-boxed.
  • Settlement default stress loss maintained within the desk's risk appetite across scenarios.
  • Median settlement exception detected and actioned within on-chain finality plus a defined buffer.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Gate each OTC trade against the counterparty credit line, approve within policy, then settle via DvP/pre-funding or manage fails without releasing value early.

Frequency

Per-trade credit check and settlement workflow; continuous monitoring of unsettled exposure until finality.

1

On trade request, load counterparty tier, credit limit, utilisation, and required settlement mode.

Owner: Credit engine / OTC trader

Control: Static data from the approved credit register; stale ratings block confirmation.

2

Approve the trade only if it fits credit and concentration headroom (or obtain credit-officer override).

Owner: OTC trader / credit risk officer

Control: Limit breaches blocked by default; overrides dual-authorised and time-boxed.

3

Confirm DvP or pre-funding per tier before releasing any delivery leg.

Owner: Settlement operations

Control: Ambiguous settlement state defaults to hold—never release value early.

4

Monitor settlement to on-chain/fiat finality; on late, partial, or failed receipt, hold further delivery and open a fail.

Owner: Settlement operations

Control: Exceptions raised automatically; counterparty delivery freeze until cleared.

5

Update real-time exposure and utilisation; escalate persistent fails for default management.

Owner: Credit risk + settlement ops

Control: Unsettled exposure reconciled to blotter; overdue fails escalate before next trade with the name.

Escalation

Credit-limit breaches need credit-officer approval before trading continues. Missed or partial settlement holds further delivery and escalates to head of credit risk and head of OTC. Confirmed default or stress loss above appetite escalates to the CRO, with counterparty suspension and default-management procedures.
