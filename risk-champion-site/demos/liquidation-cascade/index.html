Perpetual Futures Liquidation-Cascade Simulator

“I simulated how a single price shock triggers clustered liquidations that feed back into price — the mechanism behind every crypto flash-crash.”

Perp Liquidation Cascade Stress

Liquidation triggered

Not in horizon

Maintenance margin breach under cascade amplification

Problem

A fund runs leveraged perpetual positions across venues. Risk must quantify liquidation buffers and model the reflexive cascade when many positions liquidate at once.

Approach

  1. Model a book of leveraged perpetual positions with cross vs. isolated margin and maintenance-margin rules.
  2. Compute per-position liquidation prices and aggregate liquidation levels by price band.
  3. Simulate a price shock that trips liquidation clusters and feed forced selling back into the price (reflexive loop).
  4. Layer funding-rate carry and open-interest concentration to capture positioning risk.
  5. Stress with historical analogues (May-2021, LUNA, FTX) and report cascade loss and survival leverage.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Liquidation-cascade simulator with adjustable leverage and shock size
  • Liquidation-buffer report by position and venue
  • Max-survivable-leverage recommendation

Show in interview

  • Liquidation buffer (% to liquidation) per position
  • Cascade-amplified drawdown vs. initial shock
  • Open-interest concentration at key price bands
  • Max survivable leverage

Stack: Python · numpy / pandas · Interactive charts · Exchange/on-chain data

Sample talk track

At 5x cross margin the book looked fine, but my cascade model showed that a 12% shock tripped a liquidation cluster whose forced selling drove another 8% — turning a manageable move into a 30% drawdown. That reflexivity is exactly why I capped strategy leverage at 3x and moved to isolated margin on the crowded pair.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

A crypto fund running levered perpetual futures across Binance, OKX, Bybit and Hyperliquid has no forward-looking view of how a sharp spot move interacts with maintenance margin, auto-deleveraging (ADL) and cascading liquidations. Standard VaR understates tail risk because it ignores the reflexive feedback loop where forced liquidations push price further, triggering the next tranche of liquidations. The desk cannot answer 'at what spot level does our book, plus the market's aggregate leverage, start a self-reinforcing cascade, and how far are we from that point right now?'

Objective

Build a real-time perp liquidation cascade stress engine that computes portfolio and market-wide distance-to-liquidation, models cascade amplification via order-book depth decay, and produces actionable de-risk triggers before maintenance margin breaches occur, operating continuously in a 24/7 market.

Users

Portfolio manager running the directional and basis bookCrypto risk manager / head of riskTrading desk (execution and margin operations)CIO and investment committee for limit setting

Data inputs

  • Per-venue positions, subaccount margin balances, margin mode, and maintenance margin tier schedules
  • Real-time mark, index and last prices plus perp funding rates
  • Aggregate open interest and exchange liquidation heat-map / liquidation feed data
  • Order-book depth snapshots (top N levels) per venue for slippage/impact modelling
  • Historical shock event dataset for backtesting and liquidity-decay calibration
  • ADL queue / indicator status per position where the venue exposes it

Functional requirements

  • Ingest per-venue positions, wallet/subaccount margin balances, initial and maintenance margin tiers, and cross vs isolated margin mode, then compute liquidation price and distance-to-liquidation (in % and in ATR units) per position and portfolio-wide.
  • Model market-wide leverage using aggregate open interest, exchange liquidation-level heat-maps and estimated liquidation clusters to identify price levels where large notional gets force-closed.
  • Simulate cascade amplification: apply a shock path where each liquidation tranche consumes order-book depth, moves mid-price by a slippage function, and re-triggers the next cluster until convergence or a defined floor.
  • Run scenario library (e.g. -10%/-20%/-35% BTC, correlated alt beta shock, funding spike, stablecoin wobble) and Monte Carlo shock paths with configurable liquidity-decay and correlation assumptions.
  • Generate tiered de-risk triggers (reduce leverage, add margin, hedge with options/inverse perps) with pre-computed required margin top-ups to restore target buffer.
  • Provide 24/7 alerting when portfolio distance-to-liquidation, aggregate margin ratio, or ADL queue rank crosses thresholds, with venue-specific ADL indicator tracking.
  • Backtest the cascade model against historical events (e.g. May 2021, LUNA, FTX week, Aug 2024 unwind) and report realized vs predicted drawdown and liquidation timing.

Non-functional

  • Sub-30-second refresh of positions, margin and mark prices during volatile regimes; degrade gracefully to cached state if a venue API rate-limits.
  • Deterministic, auditable scenario runs with versioned assumptions (liquidity-decay curves, correlation matrix) so results are reproducible for the investment committee.
  • Resilient multi-venue connectivity with reconciliation of on-exchange balances against internal position keeping; flag any drift beyond tolerance.
  • Secure, least-privilege API keys (read-only for risk, no withdrawal scope) with secrets rotation.

Success metrics

  • Distance-to-liquidation computed within tolerance vs venue-reported liquidation price (<0.5% error) across all positions
  • Cascade model predicts realized drawdown in backtests within an acceptable band (e.g. mean absolute error < 20% of realized move)
  • Zero unhedged maintenance-margin breaches after trigger framework goes live
  • Median time from threshold breach to desk acknowledgement under 5 minutes, 24/7
  • Reduction in peak intraday margin utilization vs pre-tool baseline
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Continuously map open-interest liquidation clusters to stress price paths, quantify cascade-amplified loss on the fund's levered perp book, and force leverage reduction or hedges before maintenance margin or ADL can impair capital.

Frequency

Continuous 24/7 monitoring with sub-minute refresh in volatile regimes; mandatory stress re-run on any >5% spot move, OI cluster shift, or ADL-indicator change; formal review at each 8-hour funding boundary.

1

Ingest live positions, margin, marks and venue liquidation feeds; map aggregate open interest into liquidation-price clusters per venue.

Owner: Cascade engine / risk analyst (on rotation)

Control: OI cluster map reconciled to venue heat-maps; feed staleness or reconciliation break freezes new leverage adds.

2

Build a stress price path that walks through successive OI clusters, consuming order-book depth at each tranche.

Owner: Cascade engine

Control: Liquidity-decay and correlation assumptions pulled from the committee-approved versioned set.

3

Estimate cascade-amplified P&L and portfolio distance-to-liquidation under the stress path, including ADL queue rank.

Owner: Risk analyst

Control: Model liquidation prices checked within 0.5% of venue-reported levels before results are actionable.

4

Compare cascade loss and buffer metrics against leverage, distance-to-liquidation and max drawdown limits.

Owner: Risk manager

Control: Amber breach requires documented hold rationale; red breach mandates immediate de-risk.

5

Instruct the desk to reduce leverage, top up margin, or hedge with options/inverse perps; size the action to restore the target buffer.

Owner: Risk manager → trading / venue ops

Control: Four-eyes confirmation of de-risk instruction; venue and timestamp logged.

6

Verify post-action buffers and re-run the cascade path; archive the cycle for the committee log.

Owner: Risk analyst

Control: Residual gap below buffer escalated until closed; cycle not closed while red flags remain.

Escalation

Amber (portfolio within 1.5× of distance-to-liquidation buffer, or cascade-implied drawdown approaching limit) notifies the risk manager and PM. Red (maintenance-margin breach imminent, ADL rank in the top decile, or cascade loss above committee limit) pages the head of risk and CIO, authorizes autonomous leverage cut / hedge under the pre-approved playbook, and holds the crisis bridge until buffers are restored.
