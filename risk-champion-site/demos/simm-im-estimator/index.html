Problem

Under UMR, in-scope counterparties must post segregated initial margin on uncleared derivatives. Risk and collateral teams need to estimate SIMM IM, forecast it for funding (MVA), and pre-empt disputes.

Approach

  1. Ingest delta, vega and curvature sensitivities in CRIF format across risk classes (rates/FX, credit, equity, commodity).
  2. Apply SIMM risk weights and correlations within and across risk classes to a 10-day, 99% standard.
  3. Aggregate to initial margin by risk class and netting set and break down the drivers.
  4. Estimate IM under market moves and new trades to forecast funding cost (MVA) and threshold breaches.
  5. Let the user add/remove trades and see IM, concentration and the marginal IM of the next trade.

Deliverables & metrics

  • SIMM IM calculator with risk-class breakdown
  • Marginal-IM and MVA-forecast views
  • Reconciliation-style output to reduce counterparty disputes

Show in interview

  • Total SIMM initial margin by risk class
  • Marginal IM of the next trade
  • IM forecast under a market move (MVA driver)
  • Concentration of IM by bucket

Stack: Python · pandas / numpy · CRIF / SIMM parameters · Interactive UI

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

For non-centrally-cleared derivatives under the Uncleared Margin Rules (UMR), both parties must exchange Initial Margin computed via the ISDA Standard Initial Margin Model (SIMM). The desk needs to pre-trade and post-trade estimate SIMM IM to manage funding (MVA), avoid unexpected margin calls, and reconcile IM against counterparties. Without an in-house SIMM estimator, IM disputes, CRIF errors and funding surprises arise, and the desk cannot optimise trades against IM thresholds.

Objective

Build a SIMM Initial Margin estimator that consumes CRIF sensitivities, applies the ISDA SIMM risk weights and correlations across risk classes and product classes, aggregates to portfolio IM, reconciles against counterparty/AcadiaSoft figures, and feeds Margin Valuation Adjustment (MVA) funding analytics.

Users

Collateral / margin operations reconciling and disputing IMXVA desk quantifying MVA funding cost of IMFront-office traders assessing marginal IM impact pre-tradeCounterparty risk managers monitoring UMR in-scope relationshipsModel validation reviewing SIMM implementation versus ISDA specification

Data inputs

  • CRIF sensitivity files (delta/vega/curvature) per netting set / counterparty.
  • Current ISDA SIMM parameter set (risk weights, correlations, thresholds) with version metadata.
  • Counterparty / calculation-agent IM figures for reconciliation.
  • UMR in-scope counterparty list and CSA IM terms.
  • Funding curve / cost-of-funding inputs for MVA.
  • Trade static for pre-trade marginal IM computation.

Functional requirements

  • Ingest CRIF (Common Risk Interchange Format) sensitivities (delta, vega, curvature) tagged by risk class, bucket, qualifier and label.
  • Apply current-version ISDA SIMM risk weights, concentration thresholds and correlation parameters across the four product classes (RatesFX, Credit, Equity, Commodity).
  • Aggregate margin by risk class (IR, FX, Credit Qualifying, Credit Non-Qualifying, Equity, Commodity) and combine to total SIMM IM with the prescribed correlation matrix.
  • Compute the concentration risk factor (CR) adjustments to delta and vega margin where thresholds are exceeded.
  • Reconcile computed IM to the counterparty / calculation-agent figure and quantify disputes by risk class and bucket.
  • Provide pre-trade marginal IM (incremental SIMM of a new trade) to support trade decisions and IM optimisation.
  • Feed portfolio IM projections into MVA funding computation over the trade lifetime.

Non-functional

  • Strict conformance to the versioned ISDA SIMM methodology with clear version governance and effective dates.
  • Pre-trade marginal IM latency low enough for interactive front-office use.
  • CRIF schema validation with rejection and clear error reporting for malformed sensitivities.
  • Full traceability from portfolio IM to each contributing sensitivity for dispute resolution.

Success metrics

  • IM reconciliation agreement rate with counterparties above target (e.g., > 98% within tolerance).
  • Reduction in IM disputes and dispute resolution time versus baseline.
  • Pre-trade marginal IM available within the interactive latency target.
  • Zero SIMM version/methodology exceptions in model validation.
  • MVA funding estimates within tolerance of realised IM funding cost.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Compute SIMM Initial Margin from CRIF, reconcile against the counterparty calculation agent, raise and resolve disputes, and update MVA funding for UMR portfolios.

Frequency

Daily IM calculation aligned to the margin-call window; dispute follow-up until settlement; pre-trade marginal IM on demand.

1

Ingest CRIF sensitivity files for UMR in-scope netting sets and validate schema, risk-class tags and trade-population completeness.

Owner: Margin Operations Analyst

Control: CRIF schema validation with hard reject; completeness check vs. in-scope trade list.

2

Confirm the active ISDA SIMM parameter version (risk weights, correlations, concentration thresholds) against the governance register.

Owner: Risk Methodology

Control: SIMM version four-eyes sign-off and parameter-hash verification.

3

Run SIMM: apply risk weights and correlations by product/risk class, apply concentration adjustments, and aggregate to portfolio IM.

Owner: Margin Operations Analyst

Control: Risk-class aggregation assertions and IM range checks vs. prior day.

4

Reconcile portfolio IM to the counterparty / AcadiaSoft figure; decompose any difference by risk class and bucket.

Owner: Margin Operations Analyst

Control: Tolerance-based reconciliation with mandatory dispute log for breaks.

5

If outside tolerance, raise or respond to an IM dispute with supporting CRIF and SIMM drill-down; track to agreed IM.

Owner: Collateral Management

Control: Dispute ticket with aging SLA; maker-checker on agreed IM and collateral movement.

6

Issue/settle the margin call on the agreed IM and refresh MVA funding projections for the XVA desk.

Owner: XVA Analyst / Collateral Management

Control: Settlement confirmation and MVA recomputation sign-off.

Escalation

Material IM disputes past the resolution window, CRIF or SIMM-version failures, or MVA funding breaches escalate to Head of Collateral Management and XVA desk head; persistent disputes invoke the ISDA dispute-resolution procedure with legal and credit involvement.
