Wash-Trading & Spoofing Surveillance Engine

“I built a market-abuse surveillance engine that detects wash trading and spoofing from raw order/trade data — the market-integrity control every regulated venue needs.”

Problem

An exchange must detect and evidence manipulative trading — self-matched wash trades inflating volume, and spoofing/layering that fakes order-book pressure — to meet MiCA/IOSCO/MAS market-integrity expectations.

Approach

  1. Reconstruct the order lifecycle (submits, cancels, fills) and link related accounts.
  2. Detect wash trading via self-matched and circular-trade patterns between linked accounts.
  3. Detect spoofing/layering via high order-to-trade ratios, large orders placed then rapidly cancelled, and price impact around cancellations.
  4. Score and prioritise alerts, tuning thresholds to balance false positives against coverage.
  5. Produce case files with evidence timelines suitable for regulatory reporting.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Wash-trading and spoofing detectors
  • Alert-scoring and prioritisation engine
  • Evidence-timeline case files

Show in interview

  • Wash-trade volume detected vs. reported volume
  • Order-to-trade ratio and cancellation-speed outliers
  • Alert precision / false-positive rate
  • Cases escalated to reporting

Stack: Python · pandas · Pattern-detection logic · Case dashboard

Sample talk track

My engine flagged a cluster of accounts trading against each other to inflate volume on a thin token, and separately a trader placing large bids then cancelling within milliseconds to lift the price before selling. I tuned thresholds on order-to-trade ratio and markout so the alert queue stayed manageable, and each case shipped with a timeline an investigator or MAS could act on.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

On a 24/7 order book, wash trading and spoofing distort reported volume and price, mislead customers, expose the exchange to market-abuse liability, and can mask money laundering. Manual review cannot keep pace with millions of orders per day across hundreds of pairs, and undetected manipulation erodes market integrity and regulatory standing.

Objective

Deliver a real-time trade-surveillance system that detects self-trading/wash trades, layering, and spoofing across the order book, generates ranked alerts with evidence, supports analyst case management, and produces the documentation needed for suspicious-activity reporting and regulatory response.

Users

Market-surveillance / market-integrity analystsFinancial-crime and AML investigatorsCompliance officers responsible for SAR/STR filingQuant developers maintaining detection models and thresholds

Data inputs

  • Full order lifecycle events (place, modify, cancel, fill) with microsecond timestamps
  • Reconstructed order-book depth snapshots per instrument
  • Account linkage signals: KYC identity, funding source, device fingerprint, IP, withdrawal patterns
  • Historical baselines of normal cancel ratios, order sizes, and trade frequency per instrument
  • KYT / blockchain-analytics data linking on-chain deposits and withdrawals to accounts
  • Configuration: detection thresholds, typology definitions, alert-scoring weights

Functional requirements

  • Detect self-trades where the same beneficial owner (linked via KYC, funding source, device, or IP clustering) is on both sides of a fill.
  • Detect spoofing/layering via patterns of large orders placed away from touch and cancelled at high rates without execution, correlated with opposite-side fills.
  • Detect wash-trading rings across multiple accounts using account-linkage graphs and circular-trade heuristics.
  • Score and rank alerts by severity, notional impact, and confidence, with configurable, back-tested thresholds per instrument.
  • Provide analysts a case-management workspace bundling order/fill timelines, cancel ratios, linkage graphs, and reconstructed order-book snapshots.
  • Generate SAR/STR-ready case packs and support regulator information requests with exportable evidence.
  • Replay historical order-book data to tune detection logic and evidence false positives/negatives.

Non-functional

  • Near-real-time detection with alert generation within seconds of the triggering activity.
  • High recall on known manipulation typologies with a managed, tunable false-positive rate.
  • Immutable, time-synchronised event storage sufficient for evidentiary and regulatory use.
  • Scalable to peak market throughput of millions of order events per day without alert backlog.

Success metrics

  • Detection of >90% of injected/known manipulation cases in back-testing.
  • Alert false-positive rate reduced to a level analysts can clear within SLA (e.g. <20% of alerts closed as noise).
  • Median time from manipulative activity to alert under 30 seconds.
  • Time-to-file SAR/STR from case opening within regulatory deadlines for 100% of qualifying cases.
  • Zero substantiated regulatory findings of undetected systematic manipulation.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Detect wash trading, self-trading, and spoofing in near real time, open cases with evidence, and file SAR/STR when criteria are met.

Frequency

Continuous order/trade ingest and scoring; alerts triaged 24/7; model recalibration on a periodic release cycle.

1

Ingest order lifecycle and fill events; score against wash, self-trade, layering, and spoofing typologies.

Owner: Surveillance engine

Control: Thresholds version-controlled; changes only via back-tested releases.

2

Raise ranked alerts when pattern score exceeds threshold; dismiss clear noise with documented rationale.

Owner: Market-surveillance analyst

Control: Every alert reaches a disposition; dismissals sampled for QA.

3

Investigate substantive alerts via order-book reconstruction, cancel ratios, and account-linkage graphs.

Owner: Market-surveillance analyst

Control: Evidence attached to the case; four-eyes before closing high-severity cases.

4

On confirmed abuse, open a formal case, apply account actions, and place evidence under legal hold.

Owner: Compliance officer

Control: Account actions require compliance sign-off; evidence retention enforced.

5

Assess SAR/STR criteria and file within the regulatory deadline when met.

Owner: Compliance officer (MLRO)

Control: Filing decision documented; deadline tracked to completion.

6

Feed confirmed outcomes into model recalibration and threshold review.

Owner: Quant developer + surveillance lead

Control: Recall/precision tracked; model changes back-tested before production.

Escalation

Low-severity alerts stay with front-line analysts. High-severity or cross-account rings escalate to the financial-crime lead and MLRO. Confirmed manipulation with material impact, or employee/insider involvement, escalates to head of compliance and CRO, with SAR/STR to the FIU and regulator notification where required.
