OTC Counterparty Exposure & CSA Margin-Spiral Scenario

“I stress-tested bilateral OTC exposure through the CSA — showing how thresholds, haircuts and a vol spike turn counterparty risk into a funding-liquidity crunch.”

Problem

A fund trades OTC derivatives under ISDA/CSA terms with several dealers. Risk must quantify net exposure after collateral and the funding impact of a stressed margin spiral.

Approach

  1. Model a netting set of OTC trades with mark-to-market and a simple potential-future-exposure add-on.
  2. Apply CSA terms (threshold, MTA, initial amount, eligible collateral, haircuts) to derive net uncollateralised exposure.
  3. Shock market factors to trigger variation-margin calls and measure daily collateral outflows.
  4. Introduce wrong-way risk and a counterparty downgrade, and test collateral eligibility/haircut widening.
  5. Aggregate to a funding-liquidity view and compare against available liquidity buffers.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Counterparty exposure model with CSA terms
  • Margin-spiral funding-impact scenario
  • Wrong-way risk and downgrade sensitivity analysis

Show in interview

  • Net counterparty exposure after collateral
  • Peak daily margin call under stress
  • Cash buffer consumed (%)
  • Wrong-way risk sensitivity

Stack: Python · numpy · ISDA/CSA logic · Scenario engine

Sample talk track

The netting set looked comfortable at rest, but once I shocked rates and widened haircuts, variation-margin calls consumed 40% of the fund's cash buffer in three days — and the threshold/MTA left a residual uncollateralised tail. That reframes the trade from a pure market-risk question into a funding-liquidity one.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

OTC derivative counterparty exposure is dynamic and non-linear: mark-to-market swings drive collateral calls under the CSA, and in stressed markets a self-reinforcing margin spiral can drain fund liquidity precisely when funding is scarce. The desk cannot currently see netting-set exposure, potential future exposure and CSA-implied margin calls in a single scenario view, nor quantify wrong-way risk where exposure rises as counterparty credit deteriorates.

Objective

Build an OTC counterparty exposure and CSA margin-spiral engine that computes netting-set MtM and PFE, applies CSA terms (threshold, MTA, independent amount, haircuts, rounding), simulates variation-margin calls under market and credit stress, quantifies the funding and liquidity impact of a margin spiral, and flags wrong-way risk.

Users

Counterparty credit risk managersCollateral / margin operations teamTreasury and funding deskPortfolio managers using derivatives overlaysCRO and Counterparty Risk Committee

Data inputs

  • OTC trade population with terms, tenors and current MtM by counterparty
  • CSA and netting agreement terms (threshold, MTA, IA, eligible collateral, haircuts, rounding)
  • Posted and received collateral inventory and eligibility schedules
  • Risk-factor histories, volatilities and correlations for PFE simulation
  • Counterparty credit data: ratings, CDS spreads and PDs for wrong-way risk
  • Available funding: cash, HQLA and repo capacity from treasury

Functional requirements

  • Aggregate trades into netting sets per counterparty and legal agreement, computing current MtM exposure net of enforceable netting.
  • Compute Potential Future Exposure profiles (expected exposure, peak PFE) via Monte Carlo simulation of risk factors across the trade tenor.
  • Apply CSA mechanics precisely: threshold, minimum transfer amount, independent amount, eligible collateral and haircuts, rounding and call frequency, to derive collateral required and net call amount.
  • Simulate market and credit stress scenarios and roll forward daily variation-margin calls to reveal margin-spiral dynamics and cumulative liquidity outflow.
  • Quantify wrong-way risk by correlating counterparty default probability with exposure and highlighting netting sets where exposure rises as credit quality falls.
  • Model the funding impact of stressed margin calls against available high-quality liquid collateral and cash buffers, showing the collateral shortfall timeline.
  • Produce a counterparty exposure dashboard with limit utilisation (current exposure, PFE, settlement) and a stress league table of largest potential calls.

Non-functional

  • Recompute counterparty exposure and CSA calls intraday when material market moves occur, and complete the overnight PFE simulation within the batch window.
  • Faithful legal-agreement representation: CSA and netting terms captured accurately and validated against executed documentation.
  • Full audit trail of every collateral call, dispute and scenario assumption with maker/checker controls.
  • Deterministic, seeded simulations so PFE and stress results are reproducible for validation and dispute resolution.

Success metrics

  • Netting-set MtM reconciles to independent collateral-ops exposure within tolerance daily
  • Stressed cumulative margin call and collateral shortfall quantified for the top 20 counterparties each cycle
  • Zero missed or late margin calls attributable to the exposure engine
  • Wrong-way-risk netting sets identified and reviewed 100% each quarter
  • Peak stressed liquidity outflow kept within treasury's contingent funding capacity
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Revalue OTC netting-set MtM, apply CSA mechanics to issue or receive margin calls, resolve disputes within settlement windows, and stress-test funding impact of margin spirals.

Frequency

Daily MtM and variation-margin cycle aligned to CSA call frequency (often T+1); intraday recompute on material market moves; weekly PFE/wrong-way review; monthly CSA-term validation.

1

Revalue OTC trades to independent MtM and aggregate into enforceable netting sets per counterparty and CSA.

Owner: Counterparty risk analyst

Control: Trade population reconciled to the repository; IPV breaks above tolerance hold the call.

2

Apply CSA terms — threshold, IA, MTA, eligible collateral, haircuts and rounding — to derive the net variation-margin call or return.

Owner: Collateral operations

Control: Terms matched to executed legal docs; four-eyes on call amounts before notification.

3

Issue or respond to the margin notice and diary the contractual settlement deadline (typically same-day or T+1 per CSA).

Owner: Collateral operations

Control: Call register timestamps notice, acknowledgement and settlement; late items auto-age.

4

If the counterparty disputes valuation or eligibility, open a dispute ticket, exchange portfolio reconciliations, and track unresolved amounts past the settlement cut-off.

Owner: Collateral ops with counterparty risk

Control: Dispute log with maker/checker; unresolved balances escalate before the next call cycle.

5

Confirm settlement (cash or eligible securities posted/received) and update collateral inventory against the CSA schedule.

Owner: Collateral operations / custody

Control: Settlement confirmation matched to call ID; inventory eligibility re-checked.

6

Roll stressed market/credit scenarios forward to project cumulative margin outflow and test HQLA/cash cover; flag wrong-way netting sets.

Owner: Counterparty risk manager with treasury

Control: Stressed shortfall vs contingent funding plan; wrong-way names reviewed at committee.

Escalation

Disputed or late margin calls escalate same-day to the counterparty risk manager and collateral ops lead. Projected stressed collateral shortfall, confirmed wrong-way concentration, or exposure/PFE limit breaches escalate immediately to the CRO, Treasury and Counterparty Risk Committee for exposure reduction, IA renegotiation or contingent-funding activation.
