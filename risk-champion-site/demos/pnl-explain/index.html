Problem

Product control must produce a clean daily P&L and explain it against risk sensitivities. A large unexplained residual is the classic signal of a mismarked trade, stale data or model error.

Approach

  1. Capture start- and end-of-day risk (greeks) and market moves for an options book.
  2. Attribute P&L to delta, gamma, vega, theta and cross-effects via a Taylor expansion.
  3. Separate new-trade P&L, fixings/cashflows and market-data moves from the residual.
  4. Flag residuals beyond tolerance and drill into the driver (stale vol, missed trade, model).
  5. Reconcile clean vs dirty P&L and present a traffic-light explain summary.

Deliverables & metrics

  • P&L explain engine with greek attribution
  • New-trade / fixings / residual breakdown
  • Tolerance-based residual alerting

Show in interview

  • P&L attributed by greek
  • New-trade vs market vs residual split
  • Unexplained residual vs tolerance
  • Clean-vs-dirty P&L reconciliation

Sample talk track

The book made $2.1m; my explain attributed $1.6m to delta and gamma on a big underlying move, $0.3m to theta decay and $0.15m to vega — leaving a $50k residual inside tolerance. On another day a $400k residual turned out to be a stale vol surface point; catching that before sign-off is the whole purpose of the control.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Product Control must produce a daily P&L attribution (explain) that decomposes trading revenue into market-risk factors and reconciles clean (risk-based) P&L to dirty (actual/booked) P&L. Manual, spreadsheet-driven explains produce large unexplained residuals, delay sign-off, and undermine the P&L attribution test required for FRTB IMA desk eligibility. New-trade, fees, funding and market-data effects are hard to isolate.

Objective

Provide a daily P&L explain that computes Greeks-based predicted P&L (delta, gamma, vega, theta, and higher-order/cross effects), reconciles clean vs. dirty P&L, isolates new-trade/fees/funding effects, and flags residuals beyond tolerance for investigation and sign-off.

Users

Product Control analysts producing and signing off daily P&LFront-office desk heads reviewing desk P&L and driversMarket Risk consuming P&L for the FRTB PLA testFinance / valuation control for month-end closeInternal audit reviewing P&L sign-off controls

Data inputs

  • Start-of-day Greeks / sensitivities (delta, gamma, vega, theta) per book.
  • End-of-day and start-of-day market data (rates, FX, equity, vol surfaces).
  • Actual booked (dirty) P&L from the sub-ledger / risk system.
  • New-trade, amendment, fee and funding records.
  • IPV / valuation adjustments and reserves.
  • Trade and book hierarchy for aggregation.

Functional requirements

  • Compute predicted (clean) P&L from start-of-day Greeks and observed market moves: delta, gamma, vega, theta and relevant cross/second-order terms.
  • Reconcile clean P&L to dirty (actual booked) P&L and derive the unexplained residual (new/unexplained).
  • Isolate and separately report new-trade P&L, amendments, fees/commissions and funding/carry.
  • Apply materiality tolerances to residuals and generate exceptions for investigation with drill-down to trade/risk factor.
  • Support the FRTB P&L attribution metrics (e.g., Spearman correlation and Kolmogorov-Smirnov style tests) between risk-theoretical and hypothetical P&L.
  • Provide desk, book and risk-factor level attribution with day-on-day and MTD views.
  • Integrate independent price verification (IPV) adjustments into the explained P&L.

Non-functional

  • Attribution batch completion within the T+1 sign-off window.
  • Full traceability from explained P&L components to source Greeks, market data and trades.
  • Configurable tolerance and materiality thresholds by desk/book.
  • Reproducibility of the explain for audit and PLA test evidence.

Success metrics

  • Unexplained residual within tolerance on > 95% of desk-days.
  • FRTB PLA test pass (green zone) for IMA-eligible desks.
  • P&L sign-off completed within the daily SLA.
  • Reduction in manual explain effort versus the spreadsheet process.
  • Zero month-end P&L restatements attributable to explain error.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Produce the daily hypo / actual / RTPL P&L explain, run FRTB PLA tests, and set each desk’s traffic-light status before Product Control and desk sign-off.

Frequency

Daily T+1 explain and PLA traffic-light update; intensified month-end close with IPV overlays.

1

Validate start-of-day Greeks and market-data completeness; assemble hypothetical P&L, actual (dirty) P&L and risk-theoretical P&L (RTPL) inputs.

Owner: Product Control Analyst

Control: Greeks/market-data completeness vs. book population; missing-factor hold.

2

Compute Greeks-based hypo/clean P&L (delta, gamma, vega, theta, cross terms) and reconcile to actual booked P&L; isolate new-trade, fees and funding.

Owner: Product Control Analyst

Control: Clean-vs-dirty residual computation with component range checks.

3

Investigate residuals above materiality with trade/risk-factor drill-down and document root cause.

Owner: Product Control Analyst

Control: Exception log with mandatory commentary before sign-off.

4

Run FRTB PLA tests (Spearman / KS-style) comparing RTPL to hypothetical P&L over the lookback window.

Owner: Market Risk Analyst

Control: PLA metric calculation locked to the governed lookback and thresholds.

5

Assign desk traffic light (green / amber / red) from PLA results and residual quality; notify desks in amber/red.

Owner: Market Risk Analyst

Control: Traffic-light rules engine with immutable daily zone snapshot.

6

Obtain dual sign-off from desk head and Product Control; archive PLA evidence for IMA eligibility.

Owner: Desk Head / Product Control Lead

Control: Time-stamped dual approval; PLA pack versioned to the run ID.

Escalation

Residuals beyond tolerance unresolved by sign-off, or amber/red PLA traffic lights, escalate to Head of Product Control and Market Risk; persistent red-zone PLA threatening IMA eligibility escalates to the desk head and CRO with a remediation plan.
