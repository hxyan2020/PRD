Problem

Funds hold treasury and collateral in stablecoins whose par value is assumed, not guaranteed. Risk must monitor peg stability, redemption capacity and reserve quality, and pre-define depeg playbooks.

Approach

  1. Ingest cross-venue prices and compute peg deviation, volatility and time-below-peg per stablecoin.
  2. Monitor on-chain liquidity depth (Curve/Uniswap pools) and redemption/mint activity as stress signals.
  3. Score reserve quality (cash vs T-bills vs commercial paper vs crypto-collateral) and attestation freshness.
  4. Build a composite depeg-risk score with tiered alert thresholds.
  5. Define automated de-risking playbooks (rotate, redeem, hedge) triggered by score bands.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Depeg early-warning dashboard with composite score
  • Reserve-quality scorecard per stablecoin
  • Tiered de-risking playbook

Show in interview

  • Peg deviation and time-below-peg
  • DEX liquidity depth / pool imbalance
  • Reserve-quality score & attestation age
  • Composite depeg-risk score vs alert bands

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Stablecoins are the fund's settlement, collateral and cash-equivalent layer (USDT, USDC, DAI, plus yield-bearing and algorithmic variants). A depeg — as with UST's collapse and USDC's SVB-weekend wobble to $0.87 — can vaporize treasury value and cascade into collateral liquidations. The desk lacks an early-warning system that fuses peg deviation, on-chain liquidity, and reserve quality into a single, actionable signal with pre-defined playbooks.

Objective

Deliver a stablecoin depeg early-warning and reserve monitor that tracks real-time peg deviation across venues, DEX liquidity and redemption capacity, and reserve quality/attestations, combines them into a composite depeg-risk score, and triggers staged de-risk playbooks 24/7.

Users

Treasury managerRisk managerPortfolio managers using stablecoins as collateralCIO and investment committee

Data inputs

  • Real-time stablecoin prices across CEX and DEX venues
  • DEX pool balances/depth (e.g. Curve, Uniswap) and CEX order-book depth
  • Issuer reserve attestations and reserve composition disclosures
  • Redemption terms, gates and historical redemption latency
  • Fund exposure by stablecoin across treasury, collateral and LP positions
  • Market-structure signals (mint/burn flows, exchange netflows, banking-sector news)

Functional requirements

  • Monitor peg deviation in real time across CEX pairs and DEX pools, computing basis-point deviation from $1 and time-weighted deviation persistence per stablecoin.
  • Track on-chain and CEX liquidity: DEX pool depth and imbalance (e.g. Curve pool ratios), CEX order-book depth, and estimated slippage to exit a defined notional.
  • Assess reserve quality for fiat-backed coins: attestation recency, cash vs T-bill vs commercial paper mix, banking counterparties, and redemption terms/gates; assess collateral/backing mechanics for crypto-backed and algorithmic coins.
  • Compute a composite depeg-risk score per stablecoin from peg deviation, liquidity, reserve quality and market-structure signals, with clear thresholds.
  • Maintain and trigger staged playbooks (rotate to safer stablecoin, redeem at issuer, convert to fiat, reduce stablecoin collateral) with pre-computed swap routes and slippage estimates.
  • Alert 24/7 on peg deviation, liquidity imbalance, reserve red flags (banking exposure, delayed attestation) or redemption-gate signals, with severity tiers.
  • Track exposure by stablecoin across treasury, collateral and LP positions so the blast radius of a depeg is always known.

Non-functional

  • Second-level refresh of peg and liquidity data during stress; resilient to venue outages via multi-source aggregation.
  • Playbooks and swap routes pre-tested so execution is fast under stress; slippage estimates conservative.
  • Auditable scoring methodology and alert history for committee and regulatory review.
  • 24/7 on-call coverage with clear ownership; alerting independent of business hours and weekends.

Success metrics

  • Early-warning score elevates ahead of realized depeg events in backtests (lead time > 0)
  • All material peg deviations (> defined bps) detected and alerted within seconds, 24/7
  • Stablecoin exposure fully mapped with no unclassified balances
  • De-risk playbook executable within target time with realized slippage inside the estimated band
  • Zero uncontrolled losses from a depeg event after go-live
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Fuse peg deviation, DEX/CEX liquidity and reserve-quality signals into a depeg alert, then execute a pre-defined exit path so treasury and collateral stablecoin exposure is neutralized before a full depeg crystallizes.

Frequency

Continuous 24/7 monitoring with second-level refresh under stress; formal reserve-quality review weekly and on any attestation update, banking-sector event or redemption-term change.

1

Ingest multi-venue stablecoin prices, DEX pool depth/imbalance, CEX books and fund exposure by coin.

Owner: Depeg monitor / on-chain data ops

Control: Single-venue outliers quarantined until multi-source confirmation.

2

Measure peg deviation (bps) and persistence; estimate exit slippage for the fund's notional.

Owner: Depeg monitor

Control: Transient wicks filtered from persistence; slippage uses conservative depth assumptions.

3

Update reserve-quality score from attestations, banking counterparties and redemption terms.

Owner: Treasury / risk analyst

Control: Stale attestation auto-downgrades the reserve sub-score.

4

Compute the composite depeg-risk score and raise a depeg alert at the warning or critical tier.

Owner: Scoring engine / risk manager

Control: Thresholds versioned and committee-approved; every score change audit-logged.

5

Select the exit path (rotate to safer stable, redeem at issuer, convert to fiat, cut collateral use) and size it to exposure.

Owner: Risk manager → treasury

Control: Playbook and swap routes pre-approved; four-eyes confirmation before execution.

6

Execute the exit path via venue ops / on-chain swaps; confirm settlement and realized slippage.

Owner: Treasury / venue ops

Control: Slippage vs estimate reviewed; Travel Rule and whitelist checks applied.

7

Reassess residual exposure to the stressed coin and update the committee dashboard.

Owner: Risk analyst

Control: Residual tracked to target; open gaps stay escalated.

Escalation

Amber (peg deviation beyond warning bps, or reserve/liquidity deterioration) notifies risk manager and treasury and arms the exit playbook. Red (sustained material depeg, redemption gate, banking-counterparty failure or collapsing DEX liquidity) pages the head of risk and CIO, authorizes autonomous execution of the exit path, and holds the crisis bridge until exposure is neutralized.
