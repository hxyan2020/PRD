XVA Engine — CVA/FVA Pricing & Sensitivities

“I built a CVA/FVA calculator that runs a Monte Carlo exposure simulation, prices the credit and funding adjustments, and produces the CS01/IR sensitivities the XVA desk hedges.”

XVA Engine — CVA / FVA (toy)

CVA ≈ EE × PD × LGD; FVA applies the funding spread to expected uncollateralised exposure. Pedagogical only — production engines use full MC paths and curve bootstraps.

Problem

Every uncollateralised derivative carries counterparty-credit (CVA) and funding (FVA) costs. The desk must price these at inception and hedge their sensitivities; risk must reproduce and challenge the numbers.

Approach

  1. Simulate netting-set exposure paths (expected exposure / EPE) under a calibrated rates model.
  2. Bootstrap counterparty hazard rates from CDS spreads and compute CVA = LGD × ∫ EE(t) dPD(t).
  3. Add FVA from the funding spread applied to the expected uncollateralised exposure profile.
  4. Generate sensitivities (credit CS01, IR delta) via bump-and-revalue for hedging.
  5. Show the impact of a CSA/collateral and netting on the total XVA charge.

Deliverables & metrics

  • CVA/FVA calculator with exposure simulation
  • Sensitivity (CS01 / IR delta) output for hedging
  • Collateral/netting impact analysis

Show in interview

  • CVA and FVA in bps of notional
  • Expected-exposure profile (peak & EPE)
  • CS01 / IR delta for hedging
  • XVA reduction under CSA / netting

Stack: Python · numpy / scipy · QuantLib (optional) · Monte Carlo · Plotly

Sample talk track

For an uncollateralised 10y swap, CVA came out around 35bp of notional, dominated by the mid-life exposure hump. When I switched on a daily-VM CSA the charge collapsed by ~85%, which is exactly the trade-off the desk weighs. I also produced the CS01 so I could show how the XVA desk would hedge the counterparty spread with single-name or index CDS.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The XVA desk must price and risk-manage Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) and Funding Valuation Adjustment (FVA) across the derivatives portfolio, but exposure simulation, hazard-rate calibration from CDS, and sensitivity generation for hedging (CS01, IR delta) are fragmented. This makes daily CVA/FVA P&L hard to explain, hedging ineffective, and new-trade XVA pricing slow, exposing the desk to unhedged credit-spread and funding risk.

Objective

Deliver an XVA engine that simulates counterparty exposure, calibrates hazard rates from CDS/credit curves, computes CVA and FVA (and their incremental values for pricing), and generates hedging sensitivities (CS01, IR delta) with daily P&L attribution for the XVA book.

Users

XVA traders pricing and hedging CVA/FVAXVA quants / risk analysts producing sensitivities and P&L explainFront-office desks requesting incremental XVA for new tradesProduct Control validating XVA P&L and reservesCVA capital team linking XVA to regulatory CVA capital

Data inputs

  • Counterparty CDS spreads / credit curves and the proxy-mapping hierarchy for illiquid names.
  • Simulated exposure profiles (EE/EPE) and CSA/collateral terms.
  • Discount / OIS curves and the treasury funding curve for FVA.
  • Recovery / LGD assumptions per counterparty seniority.
  • Trade population and netting-set mapping.
  • Market data for IR and credit sensitivity bumping.

Functional requirements

  • Simulate netting-set exposure profiles (EE/EPE) consistent with the counterparty exposure engine and CSA/collateral terms.
  • Calibrate hazard rates / survival probabilities by bootstrapping counterparty CDS spreads (or proxy curves under the mapping hierarchy).
  • Compute unilateral/bilateral CVA (and DVA where applicable) integrating discounted expected exposure against default probability and LGD.
  • Compute FVA from expected funding exposure and the funding spread, consistent with the treasury funding curve.
  • Generate hedging sensitivities: CS01 (credit-spread delta) per counterparty/tenor and IR delta across the curve.
  • Provide incremental / marginal CVA and FVA for pre-trade pricing and RWA-aware decisions.
  • Produce daily XVA P&L attribution splitting moves into exposure, credit spread (CS01), rates, funding, new trades and passage of time.

Non-functional

  • Consistency of exposure simulation between the XVA engine and the CCR/EEPE engine to avoid double methodologies.
  • Sufficient Monte Carlo accuracy for stable CVA and sensitivity estimates within tolerance.
  • Performance to deliver overnight XVA sensitivities and same-day incremental pricing.
  • Auditability of curve calibration, proxy mapping and sensitivity generation for validation.

Success metrics

  • Daily XVA P&L explained to > 90% by the attribution engine (low unexplained residual).
  • Hedge effectiveness: realised CS01/IR-delta hedges track modelled sensitivities within tolerance.
  • Incremental XVA pricing turnaround within the front-office SLA.
  • CVA reserve reconciliation to Product Control within tolerance.
  • Zero material calibration/mapping exceptions in model validation.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Combine EE exposure profiles with credit and funding curves to compute CVA, DVA and FVA, generate hedge sensitivities, and rebalance the XVA book under daily P&L controls.

Frequency

Daily overnight XVA valuation and sensitivity run; intraday incremental CVA/FVA pricing on demand for new trades.

1

Bootstrap hazard rates from CDS (or approved proxies) and confirm LGD/seniority assumptions; lock the treasury funding curve for FVA.

Owner: XVA Quant / Analyst

Control: Curve calibration diagnostics and proxy-mapping approval check.

2

Pull EE/EPE profiles from the CCR exposure engine and confirm CSA/collateral consistency before valuation.

Owner: XVA Analyst

Control: Exposure reconciliation vs. CCR engine within tolerance.

3

Integrate discounted EE against default probabilities and LGD to compute CVA (and DVA where bilateral); compute FVA from expected funding exposure × funding spread.

Owner: XVA Analyst

Control: Component sanity checks; CVA/DVA/FVA roll-forward vs. prior day.

4

Generate CS01 and IR-delta hedge grids; run daily XVA P&L attribution across exposure, credit, rates, funding, new trades and theta.

Owner: XVA Analyst

Control: P&L residual tolerance; mandatory commentary on breaches.

5

Reconcile CVA/DVA/FVA reserves to Product Control’s independent valuation.

Owner: Product Control

Control: Independent reserve reconciliation with break log.

6

Execute/rebalance CS01 and IR hedges per desk policy and book hedge P&L.

Owner: XVA Trader

Control: Hedge-effectiveness monitoring and trade authorisation.

Escalation

Large unexplained XVA P&L residuals, calibration/proxy failures, or reserve breaks escalate to Head of XVA and Product Control; material hedge slippage or funding-curve dislocations escalate to the desk head and treasury with a documented action plan.
