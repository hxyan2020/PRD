Multi-Asset Macro Scenario & Historical Stress Engine

“I built a scenario engine that reprices the whole book under 2008, COVID and a rate-shock scenario — and reverse-engineers the scenario that would breach the fund's loss limit.”

Problem

Boards and clients want to know 'what happens to us in the next crisis'. Risk must reprice a multi-asset portfolio under named historical and hypothetical macro scenarios and run reverse stress testing.

Approach

  1. Define a library of historical (GFC 2008, COVID 2020, 2022 rate shock) and hypothetical scenarios as risk-factor shocks.
  2. Propagate shocks through factor sensitivities and full/partial revaluation to portfolio P&L.
  3. Model second-round effects: liquidity haircuts, correlation breakdown and margin calls.
  4. Rank scenarios by impact and attribute losses to positions and factors.
  5. Add reverse stress testing to solve for the shock that hits a defined loss/limit threshold.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Scenario library with named macro shocks
  • Portfolio P&L impact and loss attribution
  • Reverse stress test breaking-scenario report

Show in interview

  • P&L under each named scenario
  • Loss attribution by position / factor
  • Correlation-breakdown impact
  • Reverse-stress breaking shock

Stack: Python · pandas / numpy · Scenario config (YAML/JSON) · Plotly

Sample talk track

The COVID replay cost the book 14% with correlations converging to one, which broke the assumed diversification. Reverse stress testing then showed a simultaneous 150bp rates and 20% equity shock would hit the -10% board limit — a concrete, defensible number I could put in front of the risk committee.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

VaR captures normal-market losses but understates tail risk from correlated macro shocks. Portfolio managers need to know how the multi-asset book behaves under severe, coherent scenarios (a repeat of the GFC, the COVID crash, a sharp rate shock) via full revaluation, and to run reverse stress to find the scenarios that would breach capital or liquidity thresholds — capability current tools do not provide consistently.

Objective

Build a multi-asset macro scenario and historical stress engine that applies named historical and hypothetical scenarios to the full portfolio via full revaluation, aggregates P&L by asset class and risk factor, supports factor-shock and reverse-stress analysis, and produces a governed stress pack for the risk committee.

Users

Market risk managersMulti-asset portfolio managersCRO and Risk CommitteeBoard and regulatory reporting teamsModel validation team

Data inputs

  • Full portfolio positions across asset classes and legal entities
  • Pricing models, curves, surfaces and market data for full revaluation
  • Historical scenario calibrations (factor moves from named episodes)
  • User-defined hypothetical factor-shock definitions
  • Risk appetite, capital and liquidity thresholds
  • VaR/ES results for comparison and coherence checks

Functional requirements

  • Maintain a governed scenario library of named historical episodes (e.g. 2008 GFC, 2020 COVID crash, 2013 taper tantrum, 2022 rate shock) with calibrated factor moves and holding periods.
  • Support hypothetical/forward-looking scenarios via user-defined factor shocks (rates parallel/steepener, credit spread widening, equity drawdown, FX moves, vol spikes).
  • Revalue the full portfolio under each scenario using full revaluation for non-linear instruments (options, structured, convex products) rather than sensitivity approximation.
  • Aggregate scenario P&L by asset class, strategy, desk and risk factor, and rank the worst contributors and hedges.
  • Run reverse stress testing to solve for the scenario or factor-move magnitude that produces a target loss (e.g. that breaches capital or liquidity limits).
  • Compare scenario losses to VaR/ES and to risk appetite and capital/liquidity thresholds.
  • Produce a stress-testing pack with scenario narratives, results, worst-case attribution and recommended mitigating actions.

Non-functional

  • Complete the full-revaluation stress batch across the scenario library within the reporting window (target < 60 minutes).
  • Full revaluation accuracy for non-linear books, with documented pricing models and validation.
  • Reproducibility and governance: every scenario definition, calibration and result versioned and auditable.
  • Scenario library governed with committee approval for additions, retirements and recalibration.

Success metrics

  • Stress pack delivered for every scheduled risk committee and ad-hoc market event
  • 100% of the portfolio, including non-linear instruments, revalued under each scenario
  • Reverse-stress scenarios identified for each key capital/liquidity threshold
  • Scenario results reconcile with VaR/ES in overlapping regions within tolerance
  • Mitigating actions from stress results tracked to completion
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Design coherent macro shocks, full-revalue the multi-asset book, reverse-stress to capital and liquidity breach points, and deliver a board/risk-committee stress pack with mitigations.

Frequency

Monthly full scenario suite for the risk committee; weekly key-scenario monitoring; ad-hoc shock design when markets gap or new material positions land.

1

Design or refresh the shock set: select named historical episodes and craft hypothetical factor shocks (rates, credit, equity, FX, vol) with coherent holding periods.

Owner: Market risk manager with methodology

Control: Only committee-approved library entries plus documented ad-hoc shocks; narratives and calibrations versioned.

2

Confirm full positions and pricing inputs (curves, surfaces, models) cover the book, including non-linear instruments.

Owner: Market risk analyst

Control: Completeness vs IBOR; full-revaluation path confirmed for options and structured products.

3

Full-revalue under each scenario and aggregate P&L by asset class, strategy and risk factor; rank worst contributors and hedges.

Owner: Market risk analyst

Control: Pricing-model validation; run ID tied to scenario and market-data versions.

4

Run reverse stress to solve for factor-move magnitudes that breach capital or liquidity thresholds; sense-check plausibility.

Owner: Senior market risk analyst

Control: Methodology documented; implausible solutions flagged before pack inclusion.

5

Compare scenario and reverse-stress losses to VaR/ES and risk appetite; agree mitigations (hedges, de-risk, contingency) with PMs.

Owner: Market risk manager with portfolio managers

Control: Appetite breaches enter the risk register; mitigations have owners and target dates.

6

Assemble and circulate the board/risk-committee stress pack: narratives, results, reverse-stress findings and action tracker.

Owner: Market risk manager

Control: Signed, versioned pack; actions tracked to closure after the meeting.

Escalation

Losses approaching appetite escalate to the market risk manager and PMs for hedge review. Scenario or reverse-stress breaches of capital, liquidity or appetite escalate immediately to the CRO and Risk Committee (and board if material), with mandated de-risking or contingency actions and regulator notification where required.
