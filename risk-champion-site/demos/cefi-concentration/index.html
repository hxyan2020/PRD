Problem

A fund holds assets across multiple exchanges, lenders and custodians. Risk must set per-venue limits and detect solvency stress early using on-chain signals.

Approach

  1. Score each venue on regulatory status, custody/segregation model, ownership transparency and incident history.
  2. Track on-chain exchange reserves and large outflows as an early-warning signal.
  3. Set per-venue exposure limits and concentration thresholds with diversification rules.
  4. Model contagion — what happens to the portfolio if the largest counterparty fails.
  5. Define a de-risking playbook with automated withdrawal triggers.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Counterparty scorecard with per-venue limits
  • On-chain reserve/outflow early-warning dashboard
  • Contagion and de-risking playbook

Show in interview

  • Exposure by venue vs. limit
  • On-chain reserve drawdown alerts
  • Concentration (Herfindahl) across counterparties
  • Portfolio loss under top-counterparty failure

Stack: Python · On-chain APIs / SQL · Dashboard · Scorecard model

Sample talk track

My monitor tracked exchange reserves on-chain, so when a venue's stablecoin reserves dropped 20% in a day against rising withdrawal requests, it fired an amber alert and my playbook cut exposure below the limit within hours. The scorecard also downgraded any venue without genuine segregation — the single control that separated survivors from casualties in 2022.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The fund holds trading balances, collateral and earn positions across multiple centralized venues (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Deribit) and lenders. After FTX, single-venue concentration is an existential counterparty risk, yet exposure is tracked in spreadsheets that miss intraday balance moves, ignore venue credit quality, and provide no objective concentration metric. Management cannot answer 'how concentrated are we, is it within appetite, and which venue should we trim first?'

Objective

Deliver a CeFi exposure concentration heat-map that aggregates all venue exposure in real time, expresses concentration via Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) and top-N share, weights exposure by a venue quality score, and enforces per-venue and aggregate limits with clear reallocation guidance.

Users

Head of risk / counterparty risk managerTreasury and operations teamCIO and investment committeePortfolio managers allocating collateral across venues

Data inputs

  • Per-venue balances across spot, derivatives collateral, earn/staking and receivables
  • Venue quality inputs: proof-of-reserves attestations, licences, audit reports, insurance fund size
  • Withdrawal reliability metrics (historical latency, freezes) and operational limits
  • Market-based venue-health signals (native token price, listed-entity equity/CDS proxies)
  • Total fund NAV and CeFi vs self-custody split for context
  • Committee-approved limit framework and quality-tier thresholds

Functional requirements

  • Aggregate exposure per venue across spot balances, derivatives margin/collateral, earn/staking and receivables, netting where legally enforceable, and express each as % of total CeFi exposure.
  • Compute concentration metrics: HHI on venue shares, top-1 / top-3 concentration, and effective number of venues (1/HHI).
  • Maintain a venue quality score combining proof-of-reserves status, regulatory standing/licences, audit quality, insurance fund size, withdrawal reliability and market signals (CDS-like proxies, token/equity signals), and compute quality-weighted exposure.
  • Enforce a tiered limit framework (per-venue hard cap by quality tier, aggregate HHI ceiling, top-1 share cap) with soft/hard breach states.
  • Render a heat-map ranking venues by exposure and quality, highlighting cells that breach or approach limits, with drill-down to underlying balances.
  • Generate reallocation recommendations that reduce HHI toward target while respecting operational constraints (withdrawal limits, settlement windows, minimum working balances).
  • Track exposure trend and alert on rapid build-up, quality-score downgrades, or venue-specific red flags (withdrawal delays, depeg of venue-held stables).

Non-functional

  • Near-real-time balance refresh (target under 60 seconds) with 24/7 alerting independent of business hours.
  • Full audit trail of exposure snapshots, limit definitions and quality-score inputs for committee and auditor review.
  • Read-only API keys with no withdrawal permission; secrets stored in a vault with rotation.
  • Configurable, versioned limit and quality-score methodology so changes are governed and reproducible.

Success metrics

  • HHI held below the committee ceiling and top-1 venue share below cap in >95% of monitored time
  • 100% of venue exposures mapped to a current quality score with no stale (>30 day) inputs
  • All limit breaches detected and escalated within the target SLA (minutes, 24/7)
  • Measurable reduction in effective concentration (higher effective number of venues) quarter-over-quarter
  • Zero undetected overnight/weekend concentration build-ups
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Keep CeFi counterparty exposure inside appetite by scoring venues, enforcing per-venue limits, watching on-chain reserve and withdrawal signals, and withdrawing or diversifying when concentration or quality deteriorates.

Frequency

Continuous 24/7 balance and quality monitoring with near-real-time refresh; formal counterparty review each business day; immediate emergency review on any venue red flag or on-chain reserve warning.

1

Aggregate spot, margin, earn and receivable balances across all CeFi venues; reconcile to custody and accounting.

Owner: Treasury / venue ops

Control: Unexplained snapshot deltas halt new allocations until cleared.

2

Refresh venue quality scores from PoR status, licences, audits, insurance fund and market health signals.

Owner: Counterparty risk analyst

Control: Inputs date-stamped; stale inputs (>staleness threshold) mark the score low-confidence.

3

Compute HHI, top-N share and quality-weighted exposure; compare each venue to its tiered per-venue limit.

Owner: Concentration engine / risk manager

Control: Hard per-venue breaches block new deposits to that venue automatically.

4

Watch on-chain reserve adequacy and withdrawal-latency signals for venues holding material balances.

Owner: On-chain analyst / PoR feed

Control: Reserve warning or withdrawal slowdown auto-arms the withdraw/diversify playbook.

5

On limit breach, score downgrade or reserve warning, size a withdrawal/diversification plan within venue constraints.

Owner: Risk manager → treasury

Control: Plan respects withdrawal caps and settlement windows; four-eyes approval before send.

6

Execute withdrawals to whitelisted self-custody or alternate venues; confirm on-chain receipt and Travel Rule compliance.

Owner: Treasury / venue ops

Control: Addresses whitelisted only; incomplete settlement keeps the incident open.

7

Recompute post-move HHI and update the committee heat-map.

Owner: Counterparty risk analyst

Control: Residual breaches require a dated remediation timeline escalated to the head of risk.

Escalation

Soft breach (approaching per-venue cap or HHI ceiling) notifies risk manager and treasury for planned reallocation. Hard breach, quality downgrade below the minimum tier, or an on-chain reserve / withdrawal red flag pages the head of risk and CIO, freezes new exposure to the venue, and initiates emergency withdrawal under the pre-approved de-risk playbook.
