Fund Liquidity Stress Ladder & Redemption Coverage Simulator

“I modelled whether a fund can survive a redemption run — matching days-to-liquidate against stressed outflows and testing swing pricing and gates.”

Liquidity Stress Ladder

Problem

An open-ended fund with daily dealing holds assets of varying liquidity. Risk must prove it can meet redemptions and margin calls under stress and recommend liquidity tools.

Approach

  1. Bucket assets into a liquidity ladder using ADV and stressed bid-ask/haircuts, under waterfall and pro-rata liquidation.
  2. Model the liability side with historical and hypothetical redemption scenarios and margin-call paths.
  3. Compute redemption coverage ratios, time-to-cash and residual liquidity after stress.
  4. Simulate liquidity management tools (swing pricing, redemption gates, notice periods) and quantify dilution protection.
  5. Add reverse stress testing to find the redemption level that breaks the fund.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Liquidity ladder and coverage simulator
  • Swing-pricing / gate impact analysis
  • Reverse stress test breaking-point report

Show in interview

  • Redemption coverage ratio under base and stress
  • Days-to-liquidate by asset bucket
  • Swing-pricing dilution protection (bps)
  • Reverse-stress breaking-point redemption %

Stack: Python · pandas / numpy · Interactive charts · Scenario config

Sample talk track

Under a 1-in-20 redemption plus a vol spike, pro-rata slicing left the fund short on day two, but waterfall liquidation plus a 2% swing factor closed the gap and protected remaining investors. Reverse stress testing showed the fund breaks at a 28% single-day redemption — which directly informs the gate threshold.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Open-ended funds face a structural liquidity mismatch: assets take days to sell without market impact while investors can redeem at short notice. The desk lacks a repeatable way to compare time-to-liquidate against stressed redemption demand, size swing-pricing factors and decide when to deploy gates or side-pockets, leaving the fund exposed to first-mover advantage and forced fire-sales.

Objective

Build a fund liquidity stress ladder that buckets assets by days-to-liquidate under normal and stressed conditions, models redemption scenarios (including concentrated and reverse-stress), computes redemption coverage ratios, and calibrates liquidity management tools (swing pricing, gates, notice periods) with clear board-level triggers.

Users

Liquidity risk managersPortfolio managers and dealing deskFund board / management company oversightProduct and distribution (investor concentration)Compliance and regulatory reporting teams

Data inputs

  • Holdings with market values, asset class and liquidity classification
  • Average daily volume, bid-ask spreads and market-impact / transaction-cost estimates
  • Investor register with concentration, dealing frequency and notice terms
  • Historical and projected subscription/redemption flows
  • Cash, credit lines and near-cash buffer balances
  • Fund terms: dealing frequency, notice periods, gate/swing policy parameters

Functional requirements

  • Classify every holding into time-to-liquidate buckets (1 day, 2-7 days, 8-30 days, 31-90 days, 90+ days) under normal and stressed volumes using participation-rate assumptions (e.g. ≤ 20-30% of ADV) and applicable haircuts.
  • Model redemption scenarios: historical worst-case, average large redemption, top-1/top-5 investor exit, and a reverse-stress redemption that just exhausts liquid assets.
  • Compute the redemption coverage ratio and liquidity coverage horizon (liquid assets available vs stressed redemptions over 1, 5 and 30 days).
  • Calibrate swing-pricing factors and swing thresholds from bid-ask, market impact and expected net flow, distinguishing partial and full swing.
  • Evaluate liquidity tools (gates, deferred redemption, notice periods, side-pockets, in-kind) and show the residual liquidity gap after each tool is applied.
  • Track a liquidity buckets vs redeemable liabilities ladder over time with investor-concentration overlays and early-warning indicators.
  • Produce a board-ready liquidity dashboard with amber/red triggers and recommended management actions.

Non-functional

  • Refresh the full liquidity ladder daily and support on-demand recalculation when a large redemption notice is received.
  • Assumption transparency: participation rates, haircuts and impact models fully documented and version-controlled for regulator and board review.
  • Auditability of every scenario, threshold change and swing-factor decision with maker/checker approval.
  • Scalable across the fund range with per-fund overrides for mandate-specific liquidity policies.

Success metrics

  • Redemption coverage ratio maintained above the board-approved minimum (e.g. ≥ 1.0x over the 5-day stressed horizon) on ≥ 99% of days
  • 100% of holdings classified into liquidity buckets with no unclassified assets
  • Swing factor recalibrated within policy tolerance of realised trading costs (variance < 20%)
  • Zero unmanaged breaches of the liquidity early-warning triggers
  • Board liquidity dashboard delivered ahead of every scheduled and ad-hoc review
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Bucket fund assets by time-to-liquidate, stress them against redemption ladders, decide swing versus gate tools, and escalate board triggers when coverage falls below appetite.

Frequency

Daily ladder refresh; on-demand when a large redemption notice arrives; monthly swing-factor recalibration; quarterly reverse-stress and board liquidity review.

1

Refresh holdings, ADV, spreads and cash; map every position into 1d / 2–7d / 8–30d / 31–90d / 90d+ buckets under normal and stressed participation rates.

Owner: Liquidity risk analyst

Control: Unclassified assets block the ladder; stressed haircuts taken from the board-approved assumption set.

2

Overlay redemption scenarios (historical worst-case, top-1/top-5 investor exit, reverse-stress that exhausts liquid assets) and compute coverage over 1/5/30-day horizons.

Owner: Liquidity risk manager

Control: Investor concentrations reconciled to transfer-agent register; scenario library version-pinned.

3

Compare coverage ratios to early-warning and hard board minima; mark amber/red horizons on the ladder.

Owner: Liquidity risk analyst

Control: System thresholds feed the liquidity register; amber requires same-day PM commentary.

4

Decide tool response: recalibrate swing factor/threshold for flow-driven dilution, or model gates / deferral / in-kind / side-pocket when liquid assets cannot meet the notice.

Owner: Liquidity risk manager with dealing desk

Control: Follows the fund liquidity-tool waterfall; maker/checker on any swing-factor change.

5

If a red board trigger fires, prepare the contingency pack (residual gap after tools, investor fairness rationale, regulator-notification checklist).

Owner: Liquidity risk manager

Control: Four-eyes review; management-company counsel sign-off before board circulation.

6

Publish the ladder dashboard to PMs and fund oversight; table red items at the next board or convene an ad-hoc session.

Owner: Liquidity risk manager

Control: Versioned distribution; board actions tracked until coverage is restored.

Escalation

Amber coverage or rising investor concentration escalates to the PM and liquidity risk manager for cash pre-positioning. Red coverage below the board minimum, or a redemption notice exceeding liquid assets, escalates immediately to the management company and fund board, activating swing adjustment, gating, deferral or suspension with regulator notification where required.
