Problem

Funds park assets on exchanges and lenders whose solvency they cannot see. Risk must independently track counterparty exposure, proof-of-reserves signals and withdrawal readiness.

Approach

  1. Aggregate the fund's balances and open positions per CeFi counterparty into a single exposure view.
  2. Ingest published proof-of-reserves / on-chain wallet clusters and monitor reserve trends and outflows.
  3. Track withdrawal-latency and processing signals as a real-time solvency proxy.
  4. Apply haircuts to exposure by counterparty risk tier and enforce concentration limits.
  5. Trigger staged de-risking (reduce, pre-withdraw, exit) as signals deteriorate.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Counterparty exposure & concentration dashboard
  • Proof-of-reserves / outflow trend monitor
  • Withdrawal-readiness & staged de-risk triggers

Show in interview

  • Exposure & concentration by counterparty
  • Reserve trend / net outflow signal
  • Withdrawal-latency proxy
  • Haircut-adjusted exposure vs limits

Sample talk track

Rather than trust an exchange dashboard, I clustered its on-chain wallets and tracked reserve trends plus withdrawal latency as an independent solvency proxy. The fund's exposure was risk-tiered and haircut, with a hard concentration cap per venue. In a post-mortem replay of a failing lender, deteriorating outflows and slowing withdrawals would have tripped the exit trigger before the freeze.

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

The fund relies on custodians, exchanges and lenders holding its assets, but attestations are periodic, opaque and easy to game (e.g. borrowed reserves at snapshot). Post-FTX, the fund needs continuous, on-chain-verifiable evidence that counterparties actually hold sufficient assets, plus visibility into whether reserves are trending down, withdrawals are slowing, or wallets show stress — before a counterparty fails.

Objective

Build an on-chain proof-of-reserves and counterparty exposure tracker that clusters and verifies counterparty wallets, monitors reserve levels and trends, measures withdrawal latency as a stress signal, and drives a staged de-risk process when reserve or behavioural red flags appear, 24/7.

Users

Counterparty risk manager / head of riskTreasury and operations teamCIO and investment committeePortfolio managers with counterparty-held collateral

Data inputs

  • On-chain balances and transaction history across all relevant chains
  • Labelled address / wallet-attribution datasets and clustering heuristics
  • Counterparty-disclosed liabilities or the fund's own deposited-asset records
  • Withdrawal latency logs from the fund's real and periodic test withdrawals
  • KYT / sanctions screening data and Travel Rule counterparty information
  • Historical counterparty-failure case data (e.g. reserve run-downs) for calibration

Functional requirements

  • Cluster counterparty on-chain wallets via labelled datasets, deposit-address heuristics and change-address analysis, and verify reserve balances continuously across chains.
  • Compare verifiable reserves against known/estimated liabilities where disclosed (or against the fund's own deposited assets) to assess reserve adequacy and collateralization.
  • Track reserve trend over time and detect sustained outflows, snapshot-window manipulation patterns, and reserve composition shifts (e.g. into illiquid or affiliated tokens).
  • Measure withdrawal latency for the fund's own test and real withdrawals as a real-time stress signal, flagging slowdowns or failures.
  • Integrate KYT/Travel Rule screening on counterparty flows and flag interactions with sanctioned or high-risk addresses.
  • Maintain a staged de-risk framework mapping red-flag severity to actions (reduce exposure, accelerate withdrawals, halt new deposits, full exit) with pre-approved playbooks.
  • Alert 24/7 on reserve declines, adequacy-ratio breaches, withdrawal-latency spikes, wallet-cluster anomalies and adverse KYT hits.

Non-functional

  • Continuous 24/7 on-chain monitoring across all in-scope chains with near-real-time block ingestion.
  • High-confidence wallet clustering with documented methodology and confidence scoring; conservative treatment of low-confidence clusters.
  • Auditable evidence trail (block references, addresses, timestamps) for every reserve assertion and alert.
  • Segregation between monitoring/analytics and any signing keys; read-only chain access for verification.

Success metrics

  • Verifiable reserve coverage computed continuously for 100% of in-scope counterparties
  • Reserve run-downs or adequacy breaches detected ahead of public disclosure in backtests
  • Withdrawal-latency degradation flagged within the target SLA, 24/7
  • All counterparty flows KYT-screened with zero unreviewed sanctioned-address interactions
  • Staged de-risk actions executed within playbook timelines with exposure reduced to target
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Continuously verify on-chain assets against counterparty liability claims, convert adequacy and behavioural signals into a solvency signal, and cut exposure before a counterparty becomes insolvent or freezes withdrawals.

Frequency

Continuous 24/7 on-chain monitoring with near-real-time block ingestion; formal reserve review each business day; periodic test withdrawals on a defined cadence and ad-hoc on any stress signal.

1

Refresh counterparty wallet clusters and verify current on-chain asset balances across in-scope chains.

Owner: On-chain analyst / clustering engine

Control: Low-confidence cluster attributions flagged and not used as sole evidence.

2

Compare verifiable on-chain assets to disclosed liabilities or the fund's own deposited claims; compute adequacy and trend.

Owner: PoR engine

Control: Snapshot-window anomalies and composition shifts into illiquid/affiliated tokens flagged.

3

Run test withdrawals and record latency; screen counterparty flows via KYT / Travel Rule.

Owner: Treasury ops / compliance

Control: Latency vs baseline and any sanctions hit escalate immediately.

4

Publish the solvency signal (adequate / watch / impaired) from reserve adequacy, trend, latency and KYT inputs.

Owner: Counterparty risk manager

Control: Signal stage follows the approved red-flag matrix; deviations documented.

5

On watch or impaired, execute the matching exposure cut (trim, accelerate withdrawals, halt deposits, or full exit).

Owner: Risk manager → treasury / venue ops

Control: Withdrawals to whitelisted addresses with four-eyes; on-chain confirmation required.

6

Reassess residual counterparty exposure and keep open red flags escalated until cleared.

Owner: On-chain analyst

Control: Residual tracked to target; unresolved impaired signals stay with the head of risk.

Escalation

Amber (reserve trend declining, adequacy narrowing, or withdrawal latency rising — solvency signal = watch) notifies the counterparty risk manager and treasury and arms the relevant exposure-cut stage. Red (adequacy breach, sharp reserve run-down, withdrawal freeze/failure, or adverse KYT hit — solvency signal = impaired) pages the head of risk and CIO, halts all new exposure, and initiates full withdrawal under the pre-approved exit playbook.
