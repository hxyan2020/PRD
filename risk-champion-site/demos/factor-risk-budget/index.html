Factor Risk Budgeting & Risk-Parity Allocator

“I built an allocator that budgets risk — not capital — across factors, then showed a PM that 70% of their 'diversified' risk came from a single equity-beta bet.”

Problem

A multi-asset book allocates by capital weight, but its true risk is concentrated in a few factors. Risk must decompose ex-ante risk by factor and rebalance to a target risk budget (equal-risk-contribution vs mean-variance).

Approach

  1. Build a factor model (equity beta, rates, credit, FX, value, momentum) and estimate a covariance matrix with shrinkage.
  2. Decompose portfolio volatility into marginal and percentage risk contributions per factor and per asset.
  3. Implement equal-risk-contribution (risk parity) and mean-variance optimisers with constraints (long-only, turnover, box limits).
  4. Compare capital weights vs risk weights and quantify the diversification ratio and effective number of bets.
  5. Backtest the risk-budgeted allocation vs the original and report realised risk contributions.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Factor risk decomposition report (marginal & % contributions)
  • Risk-parity vs mean-variance allocations with constraints
  • Diversification-ratio and effective-bets dashboard

Show in interview

  • % risk contribution by factor (before vs after)
  • Diversification ratio & effective number of bets
  • Ex-ante vs realised risk contributions
  • Turnover to reach the target budget

Stack: Python · numpy / scipy.optimize · pandas · cvxpy (optional) · Plotly

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

Nominal asset weights hide where portfolio risk actually comes from: a handful of factors (equity beta, rates, credit, momentum) can dominate risk even when capital looks diversified. Without a factor risk-budgeting view, allocators cannot see risk contributions, cannot enforce a target risk budget, and default to naive capital weighting that concentrates risk and undermines diversification.

Objective

Build a factor risk-budgeting and risk-parity allocator that estimates the factor covariance matrix, decomposes total portfolio risk into marginal and component risk contributions, and solves for equal-risk-contribution (ERC) and mean-variance-optimal allocations against a governed risk budget with turnover and constraint controls.

Users

Multi-asset portfolio managers and allocatorsQuantitative research / portfolio construction teamRisk managers overseeing diversificationInvestment committeeModel validation team

Data inputs

  • Asset return histories and current portfolio weights
  • Factor return series (equity, rates, credit, FX, commodity, style factors)
  • Factor exposure / loadings matrix per asset
  • Estimator configuration (EWMA decay, shrinkage target, lookback)
  • Constraint set: bounds, leverage, group limits, turnover budget
  • Transaction-cost / market-impact assumptions

Functional requirements

  • Estimate the factor covariance matrix using EWMA and shrinkage (Ledoit-Wolf) estimators with configurable lookback, and expose the exposures matrix mapping assets to factors.
  • Decompose total portfolio volatility into marginal risk contribution, component (percentage) risk contribution and factor risk contributions, guaranteeing contributions sum to total risk.
  • Solve the equal-risk-contribution (risk-parity) allocation numerically and compare it to capital-weighted and mean-variance-efficient portfolios.
  • Support an explicit risk budget where the user assigns target percentage risk per asset/factor and the optimiser targets those contributions.
  • Apply real-world constraints: long-only or bounds, leverage cap, group/sector limits, and a turnover penalty relative to the current book.
  • Run scenario and sensitivity analysis showing how allocations and risk contributions shift as correlations and volatilities change.
  • Produce rebalancing proposals with pre/post risk contributions, expected turnover and transaction-cost estimates.

Non-functional

  • Solve ERC and mean-variance allocations for the full universe within interactive time (target < 10 seconds) for on-demand analysis.
  • Numerical robustness: ensure covariance matrices are positive semi-definite and optimisation converges to a documented tolerance.
  • Transparency of estimator choice, lookback and shrinkage so results are explainable to the investment committee and validation.
  • Reproducibility of every allocation run from pinned inputs, parameters and solver settings.

Success metrics

  • ERC solution achieves equal component risk contributions within a tight tolerance (e.g. ±1%)
  • Realised portfolio volatility tracks the targeted risk budget within an agreed band ex-post
  • Improved risk diversification (higher diversification ratio / lower max single-factor contribution) versus capital weighting
  • Rebalancing turnover kept within the turnover budget on ≥ 95% of rebalances
  • Allocation runs reproducible and validated with zero unresolved model-validation findings
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Decompose portfolio risk into factor contributions, surface unintended factor bets versus the target budget, and agree reallocation trades with the PM before execution.

Frequency

Weekly factor-contribution monitor; monthly strategic rebalance with the PM; ad-hoc rerun when correlations regime-shift or a factor breaches its concentration limit.

1

Estimate the factor covariance (EWMA / shrinkage) and decompose current weights into marginal, component and factor risk contributions.

Owner: Quant analyst

Control: PSD and condition-number checks; contributions must sum to total risk within tolerance.

2

Compare realised factor contributions to the target risk budget and flag concentrations (e.g. equity beta or credit dominating the book).

Owner: Risk analyst with portfolio manager

Control: Budget deviation and max single-factor contribution limits from the investment policy.

3

Tag unintended bets — factor exposures that are not part of the stated allocation thesis — and rank them by risk contribution.

Owner: Risk analyst

Control: Intended-exposure tags maintained with the PM; watchlist reviewed each monitoring cycle.

4

Solve ERC / mean-variance reallocations under bounds, leverage and turnover constraints; show pre/post contributions and estimated costs.

Owner: Quant analyst

Control: Solver convergence logged; proposals exceeding the turnover budget are blocked pending override.

5

Sit with the PM to choose which unintended bets to cut, which intentional tilts to keep, and the size of the reallocation.

Owner: Portfolio manager with risk / quant

Control: Joint sign-off on the trade list; investment-committee approval when turnover or mandate bounds are material.

6

Execute the approved rebalance and confirm post-trade factor contributions sit inside the budget band.

Owner: Portfolio manager / dealing desk

Control: Post-trade contribution reconciliation; residual gaps re-enter the weekly monitor.

Escalation

Factor contributions drifting toward budget limits escalate to the PM for a scheduled reallocation discussion. A hard concentration breach, solver non-convergence, or a proposal that exceeds the turnover budget escalates to quant research and the investment committee before any trade; unresolved model issues go to model validation.
