Derivatives Liquidation Engine & Auto-Deleveraging Stress

“I modelled an exchange's liquidation engine end to end — margin breach, liquidation, insurance-fund drawdown and auto-deleveraging — to find where a big move forces socialised losses.”

Problem

A derivatives exchange must liquidate under-margined positions without bankrupting the insurance fund or resorting to auto-deleveraging (ADL). Risk must stress the waterfall under a large move.

Approach

  1. Model a book of leveraged positions with maintenance-margin and liquidation thresholds.
  2. Simulate a price shock that pushes positions into liquidation and route them to the liquidation engine.
  3. Model market-impact of liquidations and top-ups/drawdowns of the insurance fund.
  4. Trigger auto-deleveraging when the insurance fund is exhausted and identify ADL'd counterparties.
  5. Report insurance-fund survival, bankruptcy losses and ADL incidence across shock sizes.

Deliverables & metrics

  • Liquidation-waterfall simulator (margin → insurance fund → ADL)
  • Insurance-fund drawdown & survival analysis
  • Auto-deleveraging incidence report

Show in interview

  • Insurance-fund drawdown vs shock size
  • Bankruptcy (uncovered) losses
  • ADL incidence and affected accounts
  • Liquidation market-impact slippage

Stack: Python · numpy / pandas · Order-book / impact model · Plotly

PRD

Product requirements

Problem

On a 24/7 derivatives venue, leveraged positions can breach maintenance margin within seconds during volatility. If the liquidation engine cannot close underwater positions fast enough, or the insurance fund is depleted, losses socialise onto profitable traders via auto-deleveraging (ADL), damaging trust. The desk needs to size these tail risks and ensure the liquidation waterfall behaves under stress.

Objective

Build a liquidation-engine risk and stress framework that monitors maintenance-margin health per position, models the liquidation waterfall (margin call, partial/full liquidation, insurance-fund absorption, ADL), stress-tests insurance-fund adequacy under extreme moves, and quantifies ADL likelihood so the exchange can set margin and leverage parameters prudently.

Users

Derivatives risk managers setting margin and leverage tiersLiquidation-engine / quant developersTreasury managing the insurance fundMiddle-office risk analysts monitoring margin health and ADL risk

Data inputs

  • Real-time positions, leverage, and margin balances per account
  • Mark price, index price, and funding rate per contract
  • Order-book depth and recent trade liquidity per contract
  • Maintenance-margin and leverage-tier schedule
  • Insurance-fund balance and historical contribution/drawdown
  • Stress-scenario parameters: price gaps, volatility, correlation, liquidity haircuts

Functional requirements

  • Compute real-time margin ratio per position against maintenance-margin requirements using mark price and tiered leverage.
  • Model the liquidation waterfall: margin call, partial liquidation, full liquidation, insurance-fund absorption of bankruptcy losses, then ADL.
  • Simulate liquidation execution against order-book depth to estimate slippage and residual bankruptcy loss.
  • Stress-test insurance-fund adequacy under scenarios (large gap moves, correlated liquidations, thin liquidity).
  • Quantify ADL probability and rank positions in the ADL queue by profit and leverage.
  • Recommend maintenance-margin, leverage-tier, and liquidation-price parameters that keep expected socialised loss within appetite.
  • Provide dashboards and alerts on margin-health distribution, insurance-fund balance, and projected ADL exposure.

Non-functional

  • Liquidation trigger evaluation within milliseconds of mark-price updates to avoid negative-equity accounts.
  • Deterministic waterfall logic reproducible for post-event review and back-testing.
  • Continuous 24/7 operation with resilience to mark-price feed disruption (fallback to index/mark methodology).
  • Scalable to full open-interest book without breaching the liquidation-latency budget.

Success metrics

  • Zero accounts left in negative equity beyond the liquidation cycle under normal conditions.
  • Insurance fund remains solvent across all baseline stress scenarios (no forced ADL in the modelled set).
  • ADL events kept below the target frequency, with socialised loss within appetite.
  • Liquidation slippage versus bankruptcy price kept within modelled bounds.
  • Margin/leverage parameters back-tested to contain tail loss at the target confidence level.
SOP

Standard operating procedure

Purpose

Run the live liquidation waterfall: detect margin breach, liquidate, absorb residual loss via the insurance fund, and decide ADL only when the fund is insufficient.

Frequency

Continuous, millisecond evaluation on every mark-price and position update; daily fund/margin-health review for parameter calibration.

1

On each mark/position update, compute margin ratio vs maintenance margin.

Owner: Liquidation engine

Control: Mark-feed disruption triggers index fallback and alert; evaluation latency SLO enforced.

2

On breach, issue margin call and run partial then full liquidation against book depth.

Owner: Liquidation engine

Control: Waterfall is deterministic and fully logged; slippage vs bankruptcy price monitored.

3

Absorb any residual bankruptcy loss into the insurance fund before considering ADL.

Owner: Treasury / risk manager

Control: Fund drawdown reconciled per event; balance watched against minimum threshold.

4

If the insurance fund cannot cover the loss, execute ADL against the ranked profitable/high-leverage queue.

Owner: Liquidation engine

Control: ADL ranking and fills logged and disclosed; affected traders notified per policy.

5

Log the full waterfall event, update fund balance, and alert risk on fund drawdown or any ADL.

Owner: Middle-office risk analyst

Control: Every liquidation/ADL event immutable; ADL always escalates same day.

6

Daily: review margin-health distribution and insurance-fund stress; recalibrate margin/leverage if needed.

Owner: Derivatives risk manager

Control: Parameter changes back-tested, dual-approved, and version-controlled.

Escalation

Stuck negative-equity positions or excess liquidation slippage escalate to the derivatives risk manager. Insurance-fund drawdown below minimum, or any ADL event, escalates to head of risk and treasury. Material socialised-loss risk escalates to the CRO with possible leverage cuts, new-position blocks, or a trading halt on the contract.
