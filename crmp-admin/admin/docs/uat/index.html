Execute in sequence. Do not skip ahead of failed Critical predecessors. Exit: all Critical Pass; ≤2 High waivers with written acceptance.

Step by step
  1. Open /login and sign in as risk.owner@vantagemarkets.com / risk123.
  2. Confirm Admin Home loads with department RACI visible.
  3. Sign out; sign in as viewer@vantagemarkets.com / view123.
  4. Attempt to open /admin/ai-admin — expect redirect or forbidden.
  5. Sign back in as Risk Owner (or Super Admin for remaining cases if delegated).
Pass threshold

Risk Owner reaches Admin Home; Viewer cannot operate AI Admin.

Evidence to keep

Screenshot of Admin Home + Viewer denied state; note in Audit LOGIN events.

Tally is session-local for live execution; formal sign-off belongs in Audit notes.

Exit criteria

  • All Critical cases must Pass.
  • At most 2 High cases deferred, each with written Risk Owner acceptance (WAIVE).
  • 100% of BREACH/CRITICAL samples in the window have second AI (UAT-19).
